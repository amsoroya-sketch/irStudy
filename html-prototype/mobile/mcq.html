Cardiology Arrhythmias

A 68-year-old man presents with palpitations and dizziness. His ECG shows an irregularly irregular rhythm with no discernible P waves. The ventricular rate is 110 bpm. Which of the following is the most appropriate initial management?

A
Immediate electrical cardioversion
B
IV Metoprolol for rate control
C
IV Amiodarone for rhythm control
D
Start Warfarin anticoagulation
E
Adenosine 6mg IV push
✓ Correct
This patient has atrial fibrillation with rapid ventricular response. IV Amiodarone is appropriate for rhythm control in a patient with hemodynamic instability.
