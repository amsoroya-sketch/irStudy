A 68-year-old man presents with palpitations and dizziness. His ECG shows an irregularly irregular rhythm with no discernible P waves. The ventricular rate is 110 bpm. Which of the following is the most appropriate initial management?

A
Immediate electrical cardioversion
B
IV Metoprolol for rate control
C
IV Amiodarone for rhythm control
D
Start Warfarin anticoagulation
E
Adenosine 6mg IV push
✓ Correct Answer: C
This patient has atrial fibrillation with a rapid ventricular response. While rate control with beta-blockers (Option B) is commonly used, this patient has hemodynamic instability suggested by dizziness. IV Amiodarone is appropriate for rhythm control in an unstable patient. Electrical cardioversion (Option A) is reserved for severely unstable patients. Adenosine (Option E) is used for SVT, not AF.
Reference: eTG Complete, Atrial Fibrillation, March 2025 | UpToDate: Overview of atrial fibrillation
