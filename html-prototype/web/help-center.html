Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start my first study session? ▼
Getting started is easy! From your dashboard, click on any study module (MCQ Practice, OSCE Scenarios, or EMR Simulation). We recommend starting with MCQs to build your knowledge base, then moving to OSCEs for practical application. Your progress is automatically saved, so you can resume anytime.
What's the difference between Pro and Ultimate plans? ▼
The Pro plan ($49/month) gives you full access to all MCQs, OSCE scenarios, and EMR simulations. The Ultimate plan ($79/month) adds AI OSCE examiner feedback, 1-on-1 coaching sessions, mock exam analysis, and priority support. Both plans include offline downloads on desktop.
How accurate is the AI examiner feedback? ▼
Our AI examiner (powered by Claude 3.5) has been trained on thousands of real AMC exam scenarios and validated against official marking rubrics. It provides consistent, objective feedback on your communication skills, clinical reasoning, and management planning. However, we always recommend supplementing with human feedback from supervisors or study groups.
Can I use irStudy offline? ▼
Yes! Download our desktop app (Tauri) to access content offline. You can download question packs, OSCE scenarios, and EMR cases to study without internet. Your progress syncs when you reconnect. Mobile offline support is coming soon.
How do I cancel my subscription? ▼
You can cancel anytime from Settings → Subscription. Your access continues until the end of your billing period. We don't offer refunds for partial months, but you can export your progress data before canceling.
