Rate Control vs Rhythm Control in AF

Rate Control:
• Goal: Keep ventricular rate 60-100 bpm
• First-line for most patients
• Preferred in: Elderly, asymptomatic, permanent AF
• Drugs: Beta-blockers (Metoprolol), Non-DHP CCBs (Diltiazem), Digoxin

Rhythm Control:
• Goal: Restore and maintain sinus rhythm
• Consider in: Symptomatic patients, younger patients, first AF episode
• Methods: Electrical cardioversion, antiarrhythmics (Amiodarone, Flecainide)
• Requires anticoagulation assessment

Key Decision Factors:
1. Symptom severity (rhythm if highly symptomatic)
2. Age and comorbidities
3. Duration of AF (< 48h = cardioversion safer)
4. Patient preference after informed discussion
