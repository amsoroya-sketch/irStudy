## Specialized Knowledge

### 1. Pre-operative Assessment

```markdown
PRE-OPERATIVE ASSESSMENT - ASA CLASSIFICATION

**ASA PHYSICAL STATUS:**
├─ **ASA 1:** Healthy, no systemic disease
├─ **ASA 2:** Mild systemic disease (controlled hypertension, diabetes)
├─ **ASA 3:** Severe systemic disease (poorly controlled diabetes, COPD on home O2)
├─ **ASA 4:** Severe systemic disease threat to life (recent MI, severe heart failure)
├─ **ASA 5:** Moribund, not expected to survive without surgery (ruptured AAA)
└─ **ASA 6:** Brain-dead organ donor

**PRE-OPERATIVE INVESTIGATIONS:**
├─ **All patients:** FBC, UEC, coagulation profile, group & hold
├─ **Cardiac risk:** ECG (>50 years, cardiac history, major surgery)
├─ **Respiratory:** CXR (respiratory disease, major surgery, >60 years)
├─ **Diabetes:** BSL, HbA1c
└─ **Bleeding risk:** INR, APTT, platelets (anticoagulation, liver disease)

**FASTING GUIDELINES:**
├─ Solids: 6 hours
├─ Clear fluids: 2 hours (water, black tea/coffee, fruit juice without pulp)
├─ Breast milk: 4 hours
└─ Medications: Take with sip water (except oral hypoglycemics on morning of surgery)
```

### 2. Post-operative Complications

```markdown
POST-OPERATIVE COMPLICATIONS (5 W's)

**WIND (RESPIRATORY - DAY 1-2):**
├─ Atelectasis (fever POD 1-2, basal crackles)
├─ Pneumonia (fever POD 3-5, productive cough, consolidation)
├─ PE (dyspnea, tachycardia, hypoxia POD 5-10)
└─ Prevention: Early mobilization, incentive spirometry, DVT prophylaxis

**WATER (URINARY - DAY 3-5):**
├─ UTI (dysuria, fever, pyuria)
├─ Urinary retention (post-spinal, opioids, BPH)
└─ AKI (hypotension, sepsis, nephrotoxic drugs)

**WOUND (SURGICAL SITE INFECTION - DAY 5-7):**
├─ Superficial SSI (cellulitis, purulent discharge)
├─ Deep SSI (abscess, fascial dehiscence)
├─ Organ/space SSI (intra-abdominal abscess)
└─ Management: Antibiotics, wound drainage, debridement

**WALKING (VTE - DAY 7-10):**
├─ DVT (leg pain, swelling, Homan's sign)
├─ PE (dyspnea, chest pain, hypoxia, tachycardia)
└─ Prevention: LMWH (enoxaparin 40mg SC daily), TED stockings, early mobilization

**WONDER DRUGS (MEDICATION - ANY TIME):**
├─ Drug fever (rash, eosinophilia)
├─ C. difficile colitis (antibiotics, diarrhea)
└─ Delirium (opioids, benzodiazepines, anticholinergics)
```
