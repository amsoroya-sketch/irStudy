## Specialized Knowledge

### 1. Rural Emergency Stabilization

```markdown
RURAL EMERGENCY STABILIZATION (BEFORE RETRIEVAL)

**PRINCIPLES:**
├─ ABC stabilization (airway, breathing, circulation)
├─ Telehealth support (specialist advice via video/phone)
├─ Prepare for retrieval (RFDS, NSW Ambulance aeromedical)
└─ "Scoop and run" vs "Stay and play" (depends on distance, resources)

**COMMON RURAL EMERGENCIES:**

**TRAUMA (FARM INJURIES, MVA):**
├─ Primary survey: ABCDE
├─ C-spine immobilization (hard collar, blocks, tape)
├─ **Haemorrhage control:** Direct pressure, tourniquet (limb), pelvic binder (pelvic fracture)
├─ **Shock resuscitation:** 2x large bore IV, 20 mL/kg bolus 0.9% saline
├─ **Analgesia:** Fentanyl 1-2 microgram/kg IV (titrate)
├─ Activate retrieval early (golden hour)
└─ Tetanus prophylaxis (farm injuries - soil contamination)

**SNAKE BITE:**
├─ **First aid:** Pressure immobilization bandage (crepe bandage from bite site to proximal limb, then splint)
├─ Keep still (venom spreads via lymphatics, movement ↑ spread)
├─ **Do NOT:** Wash bite, cut/suck, tourniquet, ice
├─ **Hospital:** Venom detection kit (bite site swab, urine), coagulation profile, CK
├─ **Antivenom:** If systemic envenomation (coagulopathy, paralysis, myolysis)
│   └─ Brown snake antivenom (most common), Tiger snake, Death adder
└─ Monitor: Coags 6-hourly × 24 hours (delayed coagulopathy)

**ENVENOMATION (SPIDER BITES - REDBACK, FUNNEL-WEB):**
├─ **Redback:** Local pain, sweating, nausea → Redback antivenom if severe
├─ **Funnel-web:** Neurotoxic (drooling, muscle spasms, HTN crisis) → Funnel-web antivenom (URGENT)
└─ Pressure immobilization only for funnel-web (neurotoxic), NOT redback (increases pain)
```

### 2. Telehealth Consultation

```markdown
TELEHEALTH - RURAL/REMOTE CLINICAL PRACTICE

**PLATFORMS:**
├─ HealthDirect Video Call (free, government-funded)
├─ Zoom/MS Teams (healthcare-compliant encryption)
├─ Telephone (if no video available)
└─ Store-and-forward (photos, ECG sent for asynchronous review)

**TELEHEALTH CONSULTATION STRUCTURE:**
├─ **Introduction:** Confirm patient identity, location, consent for telehealth
├─ **History:** As per normal (patient or local health worker provides history)
├─ **Examination:** Remote physical exam (family member assists)
│   ├─ Vital signs: Local nurse/health worker measures (temp, BP, HR, RR, SpO2)
│   ├─ Inspection: Camera close-up (rash, wound, throat, eyes)
│   └─ Limited examination (no palpation, auscultation unless stethoscope transmitted)
├─ **Investigations:** Point-of-care (BSL, urine dipstick, ECG if available)
├─ **Management:** Prescribe (e-prescription), advise local health worker
└─ **Safety netting:** When to seek in-person review, retrieval criteria

**MEDICARE ITEMS (TELEHEALTH):**
├─ MBS telehealth items introduced COVID-19, now permanent
├─ Bulk-billing allowed for rural/remote patients
└─ Requires patient consent documented
```

### 3. Royal Flying Doctor Service (RFDS) Retrievals

```markdown
RFDS RETRIEVAL COORDINATION

**ACTIVATION CRITERIA:**
├─ Life-threatening emergency (STEMI, trauma, stroke)
├─ Specialized care needed (ICU, neurosurgery, cardiology)
├─ Distance >2 hours by road ambulance
└─ Weather/terrain impassable by road

**RFDS CONTACT:**
├─ NSW/ACT: 1800 625 800
├─ QLD: 1300 363 400
├─ VIC/TAS: 1800 625 800
├─ SA/NT: 1800 625 800
└─ WA: (08) 9417 7444

**PRE-RETRIEVAL STABILIZATION:**
├─ ABC stabilization
├─ Specialist telehealth advice
├─ Prepare handover (clinical summary, obs chart, investigations, medications given)
├─ Prepare patient (IV access, analgesia, monitor)
└─ Clear airstrip/helipad (notify RFDS of obstacles, lighting if night)

**HANDOVER TO RFDS:**
├─ ISBAR format (Identify, Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation)
├─ Copy of notes, obs chart, ECG, pathology results
├─ Medications administered (times, doses)
└─ Family contact details
```
