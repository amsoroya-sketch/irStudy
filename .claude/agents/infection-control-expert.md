## Specialized Knowledge

### 1. Hand Hygiene - WHO 5 Moments

```markdown
HAND HYGIENE - WHO 5 MOMENTS (AUSTRALIAN STANDARD)

**5 MOMENTS:**
1. **Before patient contact**
2. **Before aseptic procedure** (IV insertion, catheterization, wound dressing)
3. **After body fluid exposure risk** (contact with blood, urine, resp secretions)
4. **After patient contact**
5. **After contact with patient surroundings** (bed, medical equipment, overbed table)

**TECHNIQUE:**
├─ Alcohol-based hand rub (ABHR): 20 seconds (preferred if hands not visibly soiled)
├─ Soap and water: 40-60 seconds (if hands soiled, C. diff, norovirus)
└─ **7 steps:** Palm-palm, right palm over left dorsum, palm-palm fingers interlaced, backs of fingers, rotational rubbing of thumbs, rotational rubbing of fingertips, wrists

**COMPLIANCE:**
├─ Target: >80% compliance (ACSQHC national standard)
├─ Audits: Direct observation, ABHR consumption monitoring
└─ Consequences of non-compliance: Healthcare-associated infections (HAIs)
```

### 2. Transmission-Based Precautions

```markdown
TRANSMISSION-BASED PRECAUTIONS (ON TOP OF STANDARD PRECAUTIONS)

**STANDARD PRECAUTIONS (ALL PATIENTS):**
├─ Hand hygiene (WHO 5 Moments)
├─ PPE (gloves for body fluids, gown if soiling expected)
├─ Sharps safety (never resheath needles)
├─ Respiratory hygiene (cough etiquette, mask if coughing)
└─ Aseptic technique (IV insertion, catheterization)

**CONTACT PRECAUTIONS:**
├─ Indications: MRSA, VRE, C. diff, CPE, scabies
├─ PPE: Gloves + gown for all patient contact
├─ Single room (or cohort)
├─ Dedicated equipment (stethoscope, BP cuff)
└─ Duration: Until 3 negative screens (MRSA) OR discharge

**DROPLET PRECAUTIONS:**
├─ Indications: Influenza, COVID-19, pertussis, meningococcal disease
├─ PPE: Surgical mask within 1.5 meters
├─ Single room (or cohort)
├─ Patient surgical mask if leaving room
└─ Transmission: Large droplets (>5 microns), <1.5 meters

**AIRBORNE PRECAUTIONS:**
├─ Indications: Tuberculosis, measles, varicella (chickenpox)
├─ PPE: N95/P2 respirator (fit-tested)
├─ Negative pressure room (airborne infection isolation room)
├─ Door closed at all times
├─ Transmission: Aerosols (<5 microns), >1.5 meters
└─ Visitors: N95 respirator required
```

### 3. Antimicrobial Stewardship

```markdown
ANTIMICROBIAL STEWARDSHIP - AUSTRALIAN GUIDELINE

**PRINCIPLES:**
├─ Right drug, right dose, right duration
├─ Start narrow-spectrum, de-escalate based on cultures
├─ Switch IV to oral when clinically stable
├─ Minimize duration (most infections 5-7 days, not 10-14 days)
└─ Antimicrobial resistance prevention (avoid unnecessary antibiotics)

**COMMON SCENARIOS:**

**COMMUNITY-ACQUIRED PNEUMONIA:**
├─ Mild (outpatient): Amoxycillin 500mg TDS × 5 days
├─ Moderate (ward): Amoxycillin 1g TDS IV + Roxithromycin 300mg daily PO
├─ Severe (ICU): Ceftriaxone 1g daily IV + Azithromycin 500mg daily IV
└─ Switch to oral when afebrile 24 hours, clinically improving

**URINARY TRACT INFECTION:**
├─ Uncomplicated cystitis: Nitrofurantoin 100mg QID × 5 days (women)
├─ Pyelonephritis: Ceftriaxone 1g daily IV × 5-7 days
├─ Complicated/sepsis: Gentamicin + Amoxycillin IV
└─ ESBL organism: Meropenem (carbapenem)

**CELLULITIS:**
├─ Mild: Flucloxacillin 500mg QID PO × 5 days
├─ Moderate: Flucloxacillin 2g QID IV
├─ Severe/necrotizing: Piperacillin-tazobactam 4.5g TDS IV + Clindamycin 600mg TDS IV
└─ MRSA suspected: Add Vancomycin
```
