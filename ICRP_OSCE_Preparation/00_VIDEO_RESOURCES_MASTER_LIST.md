## Cardiovascular Examination <a name="cardiovascular"></a>

**Related File**: `Medicine/02_Physical_Examination_Cardiovascular_Respiratory.md`

### Essential Videos (Watch These First)

1. **Stanford Medicine 25 - Cardiac Examination**
   - URL: https://stanfordmedicine25.stanford.edu/the25/cardiac.html
   - Source: Stanford University School of Medicine
   - Focus: Complete systematic cardiovascular examination
   - Why recommended: Gold standard technique from leading academic institution
   - Australian relevance: Follows international standards compatible with AHPRA/RACP protocols

2. **Oxford Medical Education - Cardiovascular Examination**
   - URL: https://www.oxfordmedicaleducation.com/clinical-examinations/cardiovascular-examination/
   - Source: Oxford Medical Education
   - Focus: OSCE-formatted cardiovascular examination with downloadable notes
   - Why recommended: OSCE-specific approach, includes common OSCE questions
   - Additional: Summary and detailed versions available

3. **Geeky Medics - Cardiovascular Examination OSCE Guide**
   - URL: https://geekymedics.com/cardiovascular-examination/
   - Source: Geeky Medics (UK medical education platform)
   - Duration: ~15 minutes
   - Focus: Step-by-step OSCE examination with video demonstration
   - Why recommended: Clear systematic approach, printable PDF guide available

### YouTube Resources (Supplementary)

4. **Stanford Medicine 25 YouTube Channel - Cardiac Auscultation**
   - Search: "Stanford Medicine 25 cardiac auscultation" on YouTube
   - Channel: @StanfordMedicine25
   - Focus: Heart sounds identification (S1, S2, murmurs, gallops)
   - Note: Multiple videos covering specific cardiac findings

**Video Quality Assessment:**
- ✅ All videos demonstrate proper 5 Ps framework (Preparation, Position, Permission, Perform, Present)
- ✅ JVP assessment technique well demonstrated
- ✅ Cardiac auscultation systematic approach
- ⚠️ Australian-specific variations: Ensure 45-degree positioning for JVP (standard practice)

**Gaps in Coverage:**
- Advanced murmur differentiation videos are limited
- Few videos specifically demonstrate Australian clinical exam format

## Usage Notes

### How to Use This Resource List

1. **Before Practicing Physical Examinations:**
   - Watch "Essential Videos" for the specific examination type
   - Take notes on systematic approach and key techniques
   - Review OSCE file in conjunction with videos

2. **During Practice:**
   - Use videos as reference for proper technique
   - Pause and practice each step
   - Compare your technique to video demonstrations

3. **OSCE Preparation:**
   - Watch videos multiple times (spaced repetition)
   - Focus on time management (most videos show realistic OSCE timing)
   - Practice presenting findings as shown in videos

### Video Selection Criteria

All videos in this list meet these criteria:
- ✅ From reputable medical education institutions
- ✅ Demonstrate proper systematic examination technique
- ✅ Freely accessible (no paywall)
- ✅ In English
- ✅ Show complete examination (not just highlights)
- ✅ Recent content (2015 or newer where available)

### Quality Ratings Legend

- ✅ = Excellent coverage, high quality demonstration
- ⚠️ = Limited coverage, may need supplementation
- ❌ = Gap in coverage, seek additional resources
