Approach to Tiredness — Delivering Diagnosis and Differentials

Source: Dr. Amir workshop — Med workshop 2 - 2026 / CLASS 4 TIREDNESS (Aug 2026 drop) · Specialty: general_practice

Purpose

The tiredness cluster has a long list of differentials, so a disciplined way of delivering the diagnosis (DX) and differential diagnoses (DDX) is needed within tight time limits.

How to State the Diagnosis

Tell the patient the most likely condition, then briefly explain it in two to three words (plain language), then give your reasons drawn from the history and examination findings.

Timing Variants

  • Four-minute history stem (with PEFE): DX and DDX are given in about 2 minutes. State the diagnosis and a brief explanation, then give the first 2–3 differentials with reasons.
  • Seven-minute history stem: DX and DDX are compressed to about 1 minute. State the diagnosis and a brief explanation, then list only the names of the differentials (no time to give reasons).
