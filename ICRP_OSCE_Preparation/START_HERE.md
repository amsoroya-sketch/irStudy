### 📚 **DOCUMENT 4: Ethics & Communication with Role-Play Scripts**
**File**: `Ethics_Communication/01_Communication_Skills_Role_Play_Scripts.md`

**What's Inside**:
- ✅ **Australian Communication Principles**

- ✅ **Station 1: Breaking Bad News** (Cancer Diagnosis)
  - Complete SPIKES framework with exact phrases
  - Setting → Perception → Invitation → Knowledge → Emotions → Strategy
  - How to handle crying ("Take your time, there's no rush")
  - Responding to "Am I going to die?" (what to say, what NEVER to say)
  - Responding to "How long do I have?" (honest but not specific)
  - Handling anger and denial
  - 90-second breakdown for each phase

- ✅ **Station 2: Informed Consent** (Colonoscopy)
  - Explaining procedure in simple language
  - Risks: Common vs rare (perforation 1/1000-2000, bleeding 1/100-200)
  - Alternatives (CT colonography, flexible sigmoidoscopy)
  - Consent form discussion
  - Handling hesitancy

- ✅ **Station 3: Angry Patient** (ED Wait Time)
  - De-escalation techniques
  - NEVER say: "Calm down", "Other people are sicker", "If you're rude I won't treat you"
  - Acknowledge → Explain (triage system) → Validate → Offer solution
  - Setting boundaries if abusive
  - When to leave and get security

- ✅ **Station 4: Interpreter Use**
  - **GOLDEN RULES**:
    - Speak TO patient, not ABOUT patient
    - Look at patient, not interpreter
    - Use first person ("I'm going to examine you" NOT "Tell her I'm going to examine her")
  - Seating arrangement (triangle formation)
  - Short sentences with pauses
  - No idioms or jargon

**Special Features**:
- Word-for-word scripts
- Australian communication style
- Cultural safety integrated
- What NEVER to say clearly marked (❌)
