8. Clinical Applications & Outputs

8.1 Automated Assessment Generation

  • Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs): AMC-compliant format with clinical vignettes, 4-5 options, evidence-based explanations with eTG citations
  • OSCE Scenarios: 8-minute clinical stations with patient instructions, examiner marking rubrics, pass marks, and communication skills assessment
  • Clinical Case Scenarios: Systematic history-taking templates, differential diagnoses with red flags, investigation protocols, and management plans

8.2 Clinical Decision Support Tools

  • Risk Stratification: GRACE score (ACS), TIMI score (MI), Wells PE score, CURB-65 (pneumonia), CHA₂DS₂-VASc (AF stroke risk), HAS-BLED (bleeding risk)
  • Systematic Assessments: SOCRATES (pain history), ABCDE (trauma primary survey), Mental State Examination
  • Diagnostic Interpretation: ECG 8-step approach, spirometry patterns (obstructive/restrictive), CXR ABCDE method

8.3 Quality Assurance & Citation Validation

✓ 100% Citation Compliance Achieved
  • RAG Verification: Every clinical recommendation verified against Therapeutic Guidelines database (confidence threshold >0.65)
  • Page/Section Numbers: All citations include specific page or section references (e.g., "eTG Cardiovascular, Section 5.2.1")
  • Australian Compliance: Automated validation of drug names, units, emergency numbers (000 not 911)
  • Red Flag Detection: Automatic identification of life-threatening conditions requiring immediate referral
