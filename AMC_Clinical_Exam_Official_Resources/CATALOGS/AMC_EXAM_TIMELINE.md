## Overview

This 6-month timeline assumes:
- **Full-time study:** 35-40 hours/week
- **Part-time option:** See adjustments at bottom (20 hours/week over 12 months)
- **Background:** Basic clinical knowledge, IMG status
- **Goal:** Pass AMC Clinical Exam with 9+/14 stations

**Weekly Time Allocation:**
- OSCE practice: 15 hours
- Textbook reading: 10 hours
- Question banks: 8 hours
- Australian guidelines: 5 hours
- Videos/other: 2 hours

## Part-Time Timeline (12 Months at 20 hrs/week)

If studying part-time alongside work:

**Months 1-2:** Foundation (Weeks 1-8 content, spread over 8 weeks)
**Months 3-5:** Specialty Study (Weeks 9-12 content, spread over 12 weeks)
**Months 6-8:** Intensive Practice (Weeks 13-16 content, spread over 12 weeks)
**Months 9-10:** Excellence (Weeks 17-20 content, spread over 8 weeks)

- Extend each phase proportionally

- More frequent but shorter study sessions
- Aim for 600+ OSCE stations (instead of 900)

## Accelerated Timeline (4 Months Intensive)

If you have strong clinical background and 4 months full-time:

**Month 1:** Weeks 1-8 content (compressed to 4 weeks, 50 hr/wk)
**Month 2:** Weeks 9-16 content (compressed to 4 weeks, 50 hr/wk)
**Month 3:** Weeks 17-20 content (compressed to 4 weeks, 50 hr/wk)

**Not recommended unless:**

## Study Tips for Success

### Daily Habits
1. **Morning:** Hardest task first (textbook reading when fresh)
2. **Midday:** Active practice (OSCE stations)
3. **Afternoon:** Questions (when slightly fatigued - simulates exam)
4. **Evening:** Light review (guidelines, videos)

### Weekly Habits
1. **Weekend:** Mock exam practice (Saturday) + review (Sunday)
2. **Monday:** Set weekly goals
3. **Friday:** Review week, adjust next week

### Avoid These Mistakes
- ❌ Passive reading without practice
- ❌ Practicing without timing
- ❌ Ignoring weak areas
- ❌ Not getting feedback
- ❌ Cramming before exam
- ❌ Neglecting Australian guidelines
- ❌ Skipping mock exams
- ❌ Studying in isolation (get study partners!)

### Success Strategies
- ✅ Practice out loud (always!)
- ✅ Record yourself
- ✅ Time every practice station
- ✅ Use Australian guidelines religiously
- ✅ Get regular feedback
- ✅ Join study groups
- ✅ Track progress (use AMC_STUDY_PROGRESS_TRACKER.md)
- ✅ Stay consistent (daily practice > cramming)
