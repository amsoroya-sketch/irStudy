## 🎯 EXAM DAY ESSENTIALS

### Exam Dates 2025
- **October 28-29, 2025** (Online)
- **November 18-19, 2025** (Online)
- **March-May 2025** (In-person, Melbourne)

### Exam Format Quick Reference
- **Stations:** 16 total (14 scored + 2 pilot)
- **Duration:** 8 minutes per station (2 min read + 8 min assess)
- **Pass Requirement:** 9 out of 14 scored stations (NEW from March 2024)
- **Expected Standard:** Australian intern level (safe, supervised practice)

### What to Bring
- **Identification:** Passport or driver's license
- **Professional attire:** Business formal
- **Online exam:** Working computer, webcam, headphones, quiet space

## 🚨 Critical Reminders

- ✅ AMC tests **AUSTRALIAN** medical practice (not international)
- ✅ eTG and AMH are your best friends
- ✅ AHPRA Good Medical Practice code is tested in EVERY communication station
- ✅ Practice out loud (minimum 600 OSCE stations before exam)
- ✅ Time yourself strictly (8 minutes, no exceptions)
- ✅ Book ARIMGSAS mock exam 4-6 weeks before real exam
- ✅ Start preparing 6-12 months before exam date
