## ✅ Exam Day Checklist

### Station Approach Template (Internalize)
1. **Read brief (2 min):**
   - What is the task?
   - What are they asking me to do?
   - Key points to cover?

2. **Enter & Greet (30 sec):**
   - Introduce yourself
   - Confirm patient name
   - Establish rapport

3. **Execute Task (6-7 min):**
   - Systematic approach
   - Cover key domains
   - Listen actively
   - Adapt to patient cues

4. **Wrap Up (30-60 sec):**
   - Summarize findings
   - Address patient concerns
   - Clear next steps
   - Thank patient

5. **Exit & Reset:**
   - Leave gracefully
   - Don't dwell on station
   - Reset for next station

### Key Exam Day Principles
- [ ] Be yourself (authentic, professional)
- [ ] Show empathy and active listening
- [ ] Speak clearly and simply
- [ ] Follow Australian guidelines
- [ ] Admit uncertainty professionally ("I would consult with...")
- [ ] Time awareness but not clock-watching
- [ ] Patient safety first
- [ ] Trust your preparation
