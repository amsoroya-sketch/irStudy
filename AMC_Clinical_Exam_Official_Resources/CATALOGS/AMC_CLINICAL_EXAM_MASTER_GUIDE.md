## Exam Overview

### AMC Clinical Examination Format (2024-2025)

**Structure:**
- **16 assessed OSCE stations** + 4 rest stations
- **Total duration:** 3-4 hours
- **Per station:** 10 minutes (2 min reading + 8 min assessment)

**Station Types:**
- Simulated patients
- Videotaped patient presentations
- Charts and digital images
- Photographs

**Assessment Domains:**
1. History taking
2. Physical examination
3. Diagnostic formulation
4. Management/counselling/education

**Clinical Areas Covered:**
- General Medicine and specialty medicine
- Surgery
- Women's Health (Obstetrics & Gynecology)
- Paediatrics
- Mental Health
- Community and hospital settings

**Pass Requirements (UPDATED March 2024):**
- **Current:** 9 out of 14 scored stations
- **Previous:** 10 out of 14 stations
- **Note:** 2 stations are pilot stations (not scored)

**Expected Standard:**
- Australian medical graduate at end of intern year
- Safe, supervised doctor
- Can recognize risk, communicate clearly, follow Australian guidelines

**2025 Exam Dates:**
- October 28-29, 2025 (Online)
- November 18-19, 2025 (Online)
- March-May 2025: In-person at Melbourne test center

## Study Timeline & Strategy

### Recommended Preparation Timeline

**Minimum:** 4 months intensive
**Recommended:** 6-12 months
**Ideal:** 12+ months part-time alongside work

### 6-Month Study Plan Template

#### Month 1-2: Foundation Building
- Read AMC Clinical Examination Specifications thoroughly
- Watch AMC Briefing Video
- Read Talley & O'Connor (core chapters)
- Set up accounts on free platforms (AMC Wise, Geeky Medics)
- Review AHPRA Good Medical Practice code
- **Target:** Understand exam format, basic clinical examination skills

#### Month 3-4: Intensive Practice
- Complete 200+ OSCE practice stations (Geeky Medics)
- Read specialty textbooks (Paeds, Psych, O&G, Surgery)
- Subscribe to eTG and AMH
- Practice with study partners
- Complete 500+ MCQs (AMBOSS or PassGP)
- **Target:** Competency in all clinical domains

#### Month 5: Australian Guidelines Focus
- Deep dive into eTG guidelines
- Review Australian prescribing practices (AMH)
- Study Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander health
- Review LGBTQI+ health resources
- Practice Australian-specific scenarios
- **Target:** Australian clinical practice familiarity

#### Month 6: Mock Exams & Refinement
- Complete mock OSCE exams (ARIMGSAS trial exam recommended)
- Review weak areas identified in mocks
- Re-read AMC Tips from Examiners
- Final review of high-yield topics
- Practice time management (8 min per station)
- **Target:** Exam-ready confidence

### Weekly Study Schedule (Sample)

**Full-time study (40 hours/week):**
- OSCE practice: 15 hours
- Textbook reading: 10 hours
- Question banks: 10 hours
- Guideline review: 5 hours

**Part-time study (20 hours/week):**
- OSCE practice: 8 hours
- Textbook reading: 6 hours
- Question banks: 4 hours
- Guideline review: 2 hours

### Key Study Principles

1. **Practice OSCE stations regularly** - Minimum 2-3 times per week
2. **Use Australian guidelines** - AMC tests Australian practice
3. **Focus on communication** - High-yield for all stations
4. **Time yourself** - 8 minutes per station, strict timing
5. **Get feedback** - Study partners, tutors, or courses
6. **Review mistakes** - Learn from mock exams and practice errors

### High-Yield Topics (Based on AMC Exam Data)

**Medicine:**
- Cardiovascular: chest pain, heart failure, hypertension
- Respiratory: shortness of breath, asthma, COPD
- Gastrointestinal: abdominal pain, diarrhea, liver disease
- Neurology: headache, stroke, seizures
- Endocrine: diabetes, thyroid disorders

**Paediatrics:**
- Developmental assessment
- Vaccination schedules
- Common infections
- Dehydration assessment
- Child protection

**Psychiatry:**
- Depression screening and management
- Suicide risk assessment
- Psychosis
- Anxiety disorders
- Capacity assessment

**Women's Health:**
- Antenatal care
- Emergency obstetrics
- Contraception counseling
- Cervical screening
- Menstrual disorders

**Surgery:**
- Acute abdomen
- Trauma assessment

**Communication:**
