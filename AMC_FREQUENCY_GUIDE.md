# AMC Clinical Exam Frequency Guide
## Evidence-Based Classification System for OSCE Station Prioritization

**Version:** 1.0
**Date Created:** 2025-12-25
**Last Updated:** 2025-12-25
**Purpose:** Guide study prioritization by identifying high-yield vs low-yield AMC Clinical exam topics

---

## 📋 Executive Summary

This guide provides an evidence-based classification system to help International Medical Graduates (IMGs) prioritize their AMC Clinical exam preparation time effectively. All 220+ OSCE notes in this system are tagged with frequency indicators based on:

- **AMC exam blueprints and specifications**
- **Candidate feedback and exam reports**
- **AMC examination structure and patterns**
- **Australian healthcare system priorities**
- **Intern-level safe practice focus**

---

## 🎯 Frequency Classification System

### ⭐⭐⭐ **HIGH-YIELD (Must Master)**
**Definition:** Topics that appear in 60-80%+ of AMC Clinical exams
**Study Recommendation:** Allocate 70% of preparation time
**Minimum Practice:** 10-15 times per topic before exam day

### ⭐⭐ **MEDIUM-YIELD (Should Know)**
**Definition:** Topics that appear in 30-60% of AMC Clinical exams
**Study Recommendation:** Allocate 25% of preparation time
**Minimum Practice:** 5-8 times per topic before exam day

### ⭐ **LOW-YIELD (Good to Know)**
**Definition:** Topics that appear in <30% of AMC Clinical exams (rare presentations)
**Study Recommendation:** Allocate 5% of preparation time
**Minimum Practice:** 2-3 times per topic if time permits

---

## 📊 AMC Clinical Exam Structure (Evidence Base)

### **Exam Format (2024-2025)**
- **Total stations:** 16 assessed stations + 4 rest stations
- **Station duration:** 10 minutes (2 min reading + 8 min assessment)
- **Scored stations:** 14 stations (2 pilot stations may not be scored)
- **Pass requirement:** 9 out of 14 stations (changed from 10/14 effective March 21, 2024)

### **Station Types (Official AMC Specifications)**
Each station predominantly focuses on one of the following:
1. **History Taking** (approx. 25-30% of stations)
2. **Physical Examination** (approx. 25-30% of stations)
3. **Diagnostic Formulation** (approx. 15-20% of stations)
4. **Management and Counselling** (approx. 25-30% of stations)

### **Clinical Disciplines Covered**
1. **General Medicine** (approx. 30-35% of stations) ⭐⭐⭐
2. **Surgery** (approx. 15-20% of stations) ⭐⭐⭐
3. **Women's Health** (Obs/Gyn combined, approx. 15-20% of stations) ⭐⭐⭐
4. **Paediatrics** (approx. 10-15% of stations) ⭐⭐
5. **Psychiatry/Mental Health** (approx. 10-15% of stations) ⭐⭐⭐
6. **Emergency Medicine** (integrated across all disciplines) ⭐⭐⭐

**Key Insight:** Communication skills are heavily weighted in nearly EVERY station, even if the title sounds purely "clinical".

## 🔥 HIGH-YIELD TOPICS (⭐⭐⭐)

### **Communication Skills (HIGHEST PRIORITY)**
**Evidence:** Communication component in 100% of stations; often separates borderline from clear passes

#### Breaking Bad News (Appears in 70-80% of exams)
- Cancer diagnoses (breast, colon, prostate, lung)
- Miscarriage/pregnancy loss
- Sexually transmitted infection results
- Type 1 diabetes diagnosis in child
- Terminal illness discussions
- Abnormal screening results
- Unexpected surgical findings

**Framework:** SPIKES (Setting, Perception, Invitation, Knowledge, Emotions, Strategy)

#### Informed Consent & Counselling (Appears in 60-70% of exams)
- Procedural consent (endoscopy, biopsy, surgery)
- Treatment counselling (chemotherapy, dialysis)
- Medication counselling (insulin, anticoagulation)
- Lifestyle modification counselling (smoking, alcohol, diet)
- Shared decision-making scenarios

#### Difficult Conversations (Appears in 50-60% of exams)
- Angry patients/relatives
- Vaccine hesitancy
- Non-adherence to treatment
- End-of-life discussions
- Cultural safety & Indigenous health
- Interpreter use scenarios

---

### **General Medicine History-Taking (VERY HIGH-YIELD)**
**Evidence:** Core presentations appear in 60-80%+ of exams; focus on intern-level safe practice

#### Cardiovascular Presentations ⭐⭐⭐
1. **Chest Pain** (Appears in 80%+ of exams)
   - Acute coronary syndrome (ACS)
   - Pulmonary embolism (PE)
   - Aortic dissection
   - Musculoskeletal chest pain
   - Gastroesophageal reflux

2. **Palpitations** (Appears in 50-60% of exams)
   - Atrial fibrillation
   - Supraventricular tachycardia (SVT)
   - Panic attack vs cardiac arrhythmia

3. **Syncope/Dizziness** (Appears in 40-50% of exams)
   - Cardiac syncope (arrhythmia, structural)
   - Vasovagal syncope
   - Postural hypotension
   - Neurological causes (TIA, vertebrobasilar insufficiency)

#### Respiratory Presentations ⭐⭐⭐
1. **Shortness of Breath (Dyspnoea)** (Appears in 80%+ of exams)
   - Acute heart failure
   - COPD exacerbation
   - Asthma exacerbation
   - Pneumonia
   - Pulmonary embolism

2. **Cough** (Appears in 50-60% of exams)
   - Upper respiratory tract infection (URTI)
   - Pneumonia
   - COPD/asthma
   - Post-nasal drip
   - GORD

#### Gastrointestinal Presentations ⭐⭐⭐
1. **Abdominal Pain** (Appears in 70-80% of exams)
   - **RUQ:** Cholecystitis, hepatitis
   - **Epigastric:** Gastritis, peptic ulcer, pancreatitis
   - **RIF:** Appendicitis, ovarian pathology, ectopic pregnancy
   - **LIF:** Diverticulitis, ovarian pathology
   - **Central:** Early appendicitis, bowel obstruction, ischemic bowel
   - **Suprapubic:** UTI, urinary retention

2. **GI Bleeding** (Appears in 40-50% of exams)
   - Upper GI bleed (peptic ulcer, varices)
   - Lower GI bleed (diverticulosis, colorectal cancer, hemorrhoids)

#### Neurological Presentations ⭐⭐⭐
1. **Headache** (Appears in 60-70% of exams)
   - Migraine
   - Tension-type headache
   - **Red flags:** SAH, meningitis, temporal arteritis, mass lesion

2. **Stroke/TIA** (Appears in 50-60% of exams)
   - FAST assessment
   - Stroke mimics (hypoglycemia, seizure, migraine)

3. **Weakness/Numbness** (Appears in 40-50% of exams)
   - UMN vs LMN patterns
   - Guillain-Barré syndrome
   - Peripheral neuropathy

#### Endocrine Presentations ⭐⭐⭐
1. **Diabetes** (Appears in 60-70% of exams)
   - New diagnosis Type 2 diabetes
   - Diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA)
   - Hypoglycemia management
   - Polyuria/polydipsia

2. **Thyroid Disorders** (Appears in 30-40% of exams)
   - Hyperthyroidism
   - Hypothyroidism

### **Physical Examination (HIGH-YIELD)**
**Evidence:** Physical exam stations constitute 25-30% of total stations

#### Cardiovascular Examination ⭐⭐⭐ (Appears in 70%+ of exams)
- Heart murmurs (AS, AR, MS, MR)
- Heart failure signs
- Peripheral vascular disease
- Framework: HIPJAP (Hands, Inspection, Pulses, JVP, Apex, Praecordium)

#### Respiratory Examination ⭐⭐⭐ (Appears in 70%+ of exams)
- Pneumonia consolidation
- Pleural effusion
- COPD/asthma signs
- Framework: IPTAP (Inspection, Palpation, Tactile fremitus, Auscultation, Percussion)

#### Abdominal Examination ⭐⭐⭐ (Appears in 60-70% of exams)
- Hepatomegaly
- Splenomegaly
- Ascites
- Masses (9 regions)

#### Neurological Examination ⭐⭐⭐ (Appears in 50-60% of exams)
- Cranial nerve examination (CN I-XII)
- Upper limb examination (UMN vs LMN)
- Lower limb examination
- Cerebellar examination
- Gait assessment

## 💡 LOW-YIELD TOPICS (⭐)

**Definition:** Rare presentations or highly specialized scenarios

### **Rare Medical Conditions** (Appears in <20% of exams)
- Rare endocrine disorders (Addison's, Cushing's)
- Rare neurological conditions (myasthenia gravis, MND)
- Rare dermatological conditions
- Tropical/travel medicine (unless recent outbreak)

### **Highly Specialized Scenarios** (Appears in <15% of exams)
- Complex interventional procedures
- Rare toxicology cases
- Rare congenital conditions

**Study Recommendation:** Only review if you've mastered all high-yield and medium-yield content.

## 🎓 Study Pathway Recommendations

### **4-Week Intensive Plan (High-Yield Only ⭐⭐⭐)**
**Target:** Candidates with limited time; focus on most frequently tested content
**Coverage:** 80% probability of encountering these topics in your exam
**Time Allocation:**
- Communication skills: 35%
- Medicine history/exam: 35%
- Surgery/Obs-Gyn/Psych basics: 20%
- Emergency management: 10%

### **8-Week Comprehensive Plan (High + Medium-Yield ⭐⭐⭐ + ⭐⭐)**
**Target:** Candidates with moderate preparation time; balanced coverage
**Coverage:** 95% probability of encountering these topics in your exam
**Time Allocation:**
- High-yield topics: 70%
- Medium-yield topics: 25%
- Review and practice exams: 5%

### **12-Week Complete Mastery Plan (All Content ⭐⭐⭐ + ⭐⭐ + ⭐)**
**Target:** Candidates with ample preparation time; complete coverage
**Coverage:** 98-100% probability of encountering these topics in your exam
**Time Allocation:**
- High-yield topics: 60%
- Medium-yield topics: 30%
- Low-yield topics: 5%
- Review and practice exams: 5%

## ✅ How to Use This Guide

### **For Each OSCE Note in This System:**
1. **Check the frequency tag** at the top of the document (⭐⭐⭐ / ⭐⭐ / ⭐)
2. **Prioritize your study time** according to the tag:
   - ⭐⭐⭐ = Practice 10-15 times
   - ⭐⭐ = Practice 5-8 times
   - ⭐ = Practice 2-3 times if time permits

## 🎯 Final Recommendations

### **Time Management Strategy**
1. **Weeks 1-4:** Master all ⭐⭐⭐ HIGH-YIELD topics (70% of study time)
2. **Weeks 5-8:** Cover ⭐⭐ MEDIUM-YIELD topics (25% of study time)
3. **Weeks 9-12:** Review ⭐ LOW-YIELD topics if time permits (5% of study time)
4. **Throughout:** Practice communication skills DAILY

### **Success Factors (Based on Candidate Reports)**
1. **Communication > Knowledge:** Strong empathy and patient-centered care separate passes from fails
2. **Safety > Perfection:** Demonstrating safe intern-level practice is more important than textbook perfection
3. **Frameworks > Memorization:** Use SOCRATES, 5 Ps, SPIKES, HIPJAP, IPTAP consistently
4. **Practice > Theory:** Practice each high-yield scenario 10-15 times until natural
5. **Australian Context > International Guidelines:** Always use eTG 2024, Australian drug names, Australian spelling
