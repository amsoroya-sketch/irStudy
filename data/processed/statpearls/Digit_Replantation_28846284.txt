# Digit Replantation

**Authors:** Ryan E. Rebowe, Shruti C. Tannan

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

The focus of replanting any part of the upper extremity is maximizing functional outcomes rather than the simple survival of the amputated part. Functional outcomes depend on the anatomical characteristics of the injury and patient-specific factors. Anatomical considerations include the level of amputation and the degree of injury to the arterial intima and other soft tissue structures. Other considerations must focus on patient factors such as age, occupation, and cultural significance attributed to the hands.
