# X-ray Image Production Equipment Operation

**Authors:** Neeraj Khiyani, Vikramjeet Singh

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Radiology, as a specialty, is incredibly dependent on technology. As a result, radiologists and technologists need to understand the technology and the physical principles that create the advantages, limitations, and risks of the equipment used in practice. Understanding these principles allows for improving image quality and selecting the most appropriate way to create a radiograph. A radiograph is the oldest and one of the most common modalities. The general radiographic room contains a few basic components to produce X-ray beams: a tube, tube housing, generator, beam filtration system, and collimator.
