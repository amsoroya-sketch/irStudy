# Ketorolac

**Authors:** Ahmad N. Mahmoodi, Preeti Patel, Peggy Y. Kim

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Ketorolac, a nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug, is an option for the management of acute moderate-to-severe pain. Ketorolac is available in various forms, including oral, nasal spray, IV, or IM administration. Widely employed in postoperative scenarios for pain control, ketorolac is particularly effective when combined with opioids, leading to a significant reduction in opioid requirements. This synergistic approach not only mitigates the need for opioids but also diminishes the occurrence of adverse effects such as vomiting and decreased gastrointestinal motility.
