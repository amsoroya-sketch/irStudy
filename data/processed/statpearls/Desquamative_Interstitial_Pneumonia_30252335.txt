# Desquamative Interstitial Pneumonia

**Authors:** Abdulghani Sankari, Hajira Basit, Sandeep Sharma

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Desquamative interstitial pneumonia (DIP) is a relatively uncommon form of idiopathic interstitial pneumonia (IIP), classified according to international multidisciplinary consensus guidelines. The term "desquamative interstitial pneumonia or DIP" originated from studies in the early 1960s that described alveolar infiltrates resulting from alveolar epithelial cell desquamation. However, later research revealed that these infiltrates primarily consist of alveolar filling with pigmented macrophages.
