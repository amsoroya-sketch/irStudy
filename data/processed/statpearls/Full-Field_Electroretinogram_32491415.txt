# Full-Field Electroretinogram

**Authors:** Samuel Asanad, Rustum Karanjia

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

The full-field electroretinogram (ffERG) is an ophthalmologic test that provides non-invasive objective quantitative measures of the electrical activity in the retina. The ffERG represents an electrical response from the retina to a flash of light and measures global retinal function. Analysis of the ffERG waveform components can provide useful diagnostic information for distinguishing various retinal diseases, especially when the etiology of vision loss remains unclear following standard ophthalmologic clinical examination.
