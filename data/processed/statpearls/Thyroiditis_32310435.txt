# Thyroiditis

**Authors:** Maria M. Fariduddin, Gurdeep Singh

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

The term thyroiditis reflects inflammation of the thyroid gland. Thyroiditis can be classified based on the onset of symptoms, underlying etiology, and clinical symptoms. The most common cause of thyroiditis is an autoimmune disease. In the United States, Hashimoto thyroiditis is the most common cause of hypothyroidism. Thyroiditis can cause transient or permanent hypo and hyperthyroidism.
