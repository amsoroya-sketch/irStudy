# Nalbuphine

**Authors:** Devin Larsen, Christopher V. Maani

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Nalbuphine is a synthetic opioid analgesic indicated for the management of moderate to severe pain when alternative treatments prove inadequate. This educational activity reviews nalbuphine’s FDA-approved indications, proper administration, and relevant contraindications and precautions. Emphasis is placed on nalbuphine’s pharmacodynamic and pharmacokinetic properties, including its mixed agonist-antagonist profile, which impacts both therapeutic benefits and adverse event risks. The boxed warning regarding coadministration with benzodiazepines is addressed in detail, highlighting the potential for life-threatening respiratory depression, sedation, coma, and death.
