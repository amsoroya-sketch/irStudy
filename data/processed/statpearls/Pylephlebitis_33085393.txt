# Pylephlebitis

**Authors:** Jesse Hartpence, Andrew Woolf

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Pylephlebitis, defined as septic thrombophlebitis of the portal veins, is a rare but deleterious complication of an intra-abdominal or pelvic infection of any etiology. Prior to the advent of widespread antibiotic use, this affliction was almost universally fatal, but advances in imaging technology have aided in the earlier detection and diagnosis of this disease process. It is imperative to consider this in the differential diagnosis of any patient presenting with intra-abdominal sepsis with polymicrobial bacteremia since disastrous complications such as small bowel infarction, hepatic abscesses, and septic pulmonary emboli can occur.
