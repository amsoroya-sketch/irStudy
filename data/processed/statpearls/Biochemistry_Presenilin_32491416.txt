# Biochemistry, Presenilin

**Authors:** Sunita Nadendla, Shamim S. Mohiuddin

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

More than 5 million Americans have Alzheimer disease, and a subset of these cases is due to genetic disorders. This includes familial Alzheimer disease, caused by mutations in the presenilin-1 and presenilin-2 genes.
