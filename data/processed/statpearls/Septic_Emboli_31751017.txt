# Septic Emboli

**Authors:** Hend Elsaghir, Yasir Al Khalili

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Septic embolism is a vascular obstruction caused by an infected thrombus originating from a distant infectious focus that produces a dual insult: Ischemic injury to vascular occlusion, potentially resulting in infarction. Infectious insult characterized by local inflammation and possible abscess formation . The most common etiology is infective endocarditis. Osler nodes (tender, violaceous papules considered pathognomonic for endocarditis) represent embolic phenomena, and biopsy may yield the causative microorganisms. Identifying Osler nodes from Janeway lesions, typically microabscesses, remains challenging.
