# Osteoarthritis

**Authors:** Rouhin Sen, John A. Hurley

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Osteoarthritis is the most common form of arthritis in the world. It can be classified into 2 categories: primary osteoarthritis and secondary osteoarthritis. Classically, osteoarthritis presents with joint pain and loss of function; however, the disease is clinically very variable and can present merely as an asymptomatic incidental finding to a devastating and permanently disabling disorder.
