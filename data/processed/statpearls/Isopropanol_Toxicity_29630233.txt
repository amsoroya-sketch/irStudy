# Isopropanol Toxicity

**Authors:** John V. Ashurst, Thomas M. Nappe

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Isopropanol, or isopropyl alcohol, is a clear, colorless liquid that emits an odor that resembles acetone. It is a commercially available product mainly found as a 70% solution in rubbing alcohol and hand sanitizers. Unlike the other toxic alcohols, isopropanol rarely causes death and is associated with ketosis without acidosis. Treatment of isopropanol ingestions is typically supportive therapy, and ingestions are rarely lethal.
