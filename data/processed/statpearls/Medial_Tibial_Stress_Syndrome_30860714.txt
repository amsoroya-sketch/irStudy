# Medial Tibial Stress Syndrome

**Authors:** Charles J. McClure, Robert Oh

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Medial tibial stress syndrome (MTSS) is a frequent overuse lower extremity injury in athletes and military personnel. MTSS is exercise-induced pain over the anterior tibia and is an early stress injury in the continuum of tibial stress fractures. It has the layman's moniker of “shin splints.”
