# COX Inhibitors

**Authors:** Owais Qureshi, Anterpreet Dua

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

This program thoroughly explores the pivotal role of COX inhibitors in managing chronic pain syndromes across diverse disease spectrums. As indispensable anti-inflammatory agents, COX inhibitors significantly contribute to alleviating pain associated with various conditions. The program is designed to elucidate the intricate mechanisms of action, delineate essential indications, and highlight critical contraindications crucial for the effective treatment of chronic pain syndromes. Healthcare professionals participating in this comprehensive review will gain valuable insights into the adverse event profile, dosage considerations, pharmacodynamics, pharmacokinetics, monitoring parameters, and potential interactions with other medications. The goal is to equip multidisciplinary healthcare teams with a nuanced understanding of COX inhibitors, enabling them to provide optimal patient care for chronic pain syndromes and associated ailments.
