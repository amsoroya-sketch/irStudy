# Comprehensive Shoulder Evaluation Strategies

**Authors:** Matthew A. Varacallo, Youssef El Bitar, Scott D. Mair

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Shoulder pathology and dysfunction represent some of the most commonly diagnosed and treated conditions by primary care, sports medicine, and orthopedic providers.  This comprehensive review article discusses the clinical pearls and tips and tricks for delineating complex shoulder pathologies.
