# Acquired Angioedema

**Authors:** Terrell J. Swanson, Bhupendra C. Patel

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Angioedema is characterized by one or more areas of well-demarcated, non-pitting edema of deep subcutaneous tissues. Although known primarily to involve the face and intra-oral area, it can also involve the genitals, distal extremities, and intraabdominal gastrointestinal mucosa. Immediate identification of angioedema is of the utmost importance due to the potential for rapid development and progression of life-threatening airway compromise due to either intraoral and/or laryngeal edema.
