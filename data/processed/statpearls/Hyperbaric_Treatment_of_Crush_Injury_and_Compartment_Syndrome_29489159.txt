# Hyperbaric Treatment of Crush Injury and Compartment Syndrome

**Authors:** Tom Herron, Anthony Haftel, Klaus D. Torp, Jeffrey S. Cooper

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Human limbs are composed of muscle groups divided by fascial membranes into anatomic compartments. When a limb sustains trauma (ie, crush injury, fractures, repeated injections or infusions, or overuse), swelling and inflammation within a compartment may increase rapidly. Consequently, acute compartment syndrome (ACS) results when compartment pressures increase to the point that tissue perfusion is compromised.
