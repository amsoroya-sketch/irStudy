## Abstract

Bone fracture healing: is an intricate and fluent regenerative process that aims at restoring the damaged bone to its pre-injury state and cellular composition. A fracture is a breach in the structural continuity of the bone cortex, with a degree of injury to the surrounding soft tissues.
