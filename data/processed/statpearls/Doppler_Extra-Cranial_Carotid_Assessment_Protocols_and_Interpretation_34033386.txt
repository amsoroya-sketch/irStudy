# Doppler Extra-Cranial Carotid Assessment, Protocols, and Interpretation

**Authors:** Kirti Dhingra, Blake H. Salisbury, Mathew N. Chakko

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Over the past few decades, Doppler ultrasound has become a primary modality for extracranial carotid assessment because it is readily available, noninvasive, and relatively inexpensive. The sonographic evaluation of extracranial carotids aids in the screening, diagnosis, and monitoring of atherosclerotic disease, as well as postintervention analysis. The evaluation is crucial since the stroke risk increases with the degree of atherosclerotic narrowing, leading to flow disturbances. Doppler imaging provides a quantitative determination of velocity, which, in combination with grayscale imaging, provides a qualitative and quantitative assessment of plaque and stenosis. This evaluation is of utmost clinical importance for stroke risk stratification, as well as identifying indications for surgical intervention.
