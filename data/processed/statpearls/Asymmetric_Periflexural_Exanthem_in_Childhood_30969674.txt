# Asymmetric Periflexural Exanthem in Childhood

**Authors:** Meryam Chaabani, Pirthvi Raj Giyanwani, Asmahane Souissi

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Asymmetric periflexural exanthem of childhood (APEC) is a dermatosis that was first described in 1962 by Brunner et al as "a new papular erythema.’’ In 1986, Taieb et al also described this disease and then suggested the term APEC in a second publication in 1993. APEC is a distinctive and self-limited exanthem that classically affects children.[3] The precise etiology of this condition is unknown, but the hypothesis of a viral cause seems plausible, which remains unconfirmed. APEC manifests as a unilateral papular exanthem with stereotypical morphology and distribution. Usually, laboratory investigations and biopsies are unnecessary for this condition because of its excellent prognosis and self-limited character in a few weeks.
