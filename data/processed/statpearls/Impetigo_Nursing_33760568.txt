# Impetigo (Nursing)

**Authors:** Naomi M. Nardi, Timothy J. Schaefer, Maryrose O. Espil

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Impetigo is a common infection of the superficial layers of the epidermis that is highly contagious and most commonly caused by gram-positive bacteria. It most commonly presents as erythematous plaques with a yellow crust and may be itchy or painful.  The lesions are highly contagious and spread easily. Diagnosis is typically based on the symptoms and clinical manifestations alone. Treatment involves topical and oral antibiotics and symptomatic care.
