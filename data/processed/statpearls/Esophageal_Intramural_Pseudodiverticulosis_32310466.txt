# Esophageal Intramural Pseudodiverticulosis

**Authors:** Aws Alameri, Tariq Sharman

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Diffuse intramural esophageal diverticulosis is a rare disease characterized by multiple tiny flask-shaped outpouching lesions of the esophageal wall. These outpouchings represent the ducts of the esophagus's submucosal glands; for this reason, the disorder is also called esophageal intramural pseudodiverticulosis (EIP). It was first described by Mendl et al in 1960. Usually, cases present with dysphagia and food impaction in association with a proximal esophageal stricture. The diagnosis is usually made radiologically, endoscopically, and pathologically.
