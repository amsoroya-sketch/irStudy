# Opioid Equivalency

**Authors:** Mamta Bhatnagar, Jennifer Pruskowski

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Understanding opioid equivalency is essential to effectively managing cancer-related and chronic pain. Tailored for healthcare providers involved in pain management, the activity emphasizes the significance of comprehending and applying opioid equivalent dosing, especially for opioid-tolerant patients needing opioid rotation. Participants will explore the methods and principles of equianalgesic conversion between different opioids, aiding in therapeutic transitions while optimizing analgesic effectiveness and mitigating potential adverse effects.
