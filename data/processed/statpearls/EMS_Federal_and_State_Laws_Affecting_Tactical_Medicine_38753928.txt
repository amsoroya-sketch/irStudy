# EMS Federal and State Laws Affecting Tactical Medicine

**Authors:** Erin N. Snyder, Kennen Less

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Serving as a specialized branch of emergency medical services (EMS) and a subspecialty of Tactical Medicine, Tactical Emergency Medical Support (TEMS) has the primary goal of providing medical care while in a tactical environment to reduce physical and moral injury, preserve safety, and save lives while accomplishing the primary objective or mission. By reducing risk, bodily harm, and death to tactical team members, bystanders/civilians, and suspects, TEMS members achieve this goal. TEMS-trained providers include EMTs, paramedics, nurses, advanced practice providers, and physicians. They are frequently embedded within a tactical team and have direct and indirect impacts on medical care, primarily training team members how to perform buddy aid and occasionally providing direct medical care during operations.
