# Leiomyoma(Archived)

**Authors:** Ashley M. Florence, Mary Fatehi

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Leiomyomas, also called fibroids, are the most common benign gynecological tumor in premenopausal women . The economic impact of fibroids is profound, affecting an estimated 11 million women  and costing approximately 34 billion dollars in the United States annually . This article presents the general approach to diagnosis, pathophysiology, histology, and treatment of leiomyomas.
