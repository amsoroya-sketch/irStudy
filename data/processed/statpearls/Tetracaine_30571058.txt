# Tetracaine

**Authors:** Christopher M. Stringer, Preeti Patel, Christopher V. Maani

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Tetracaine is an amino-ester local anesthetic primarily administered for topical ophthalmic anesthesia during short procedures involving the eyes, ears, or nose. Additional applications include spinal anesthesia. Listed by the World Health Organization as an essential medicine, tetracaine offers a cost-effective alternative to other local anesthetic agents. Effective clinical use requires a thorough understanding of the agent’s pharmacologic properties to ensure safe and appropriate application.
