# Prevention Strategies

**Authors:** Daniel Keyes, Hussein Turfe, Joe M. Das

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Prevention in health involves intervening to avoid disease and injury. Effective strategies for preventive health rely on accurate health statistics to enhance their impact. This activity outlines different levels of prevention and highlights lifestyle and behavioral modifications as key approaches to reducing disease risk.
