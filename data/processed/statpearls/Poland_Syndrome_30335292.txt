# Poland Syndrome

**Authors:** Dawood Tafti, Nathan D. Cecava

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Poland syndrome consists of anatomic anomalies that include the absence of the sternocostal head of the pectoralis major muscle with other varied manifestations that include hypoplasia or absence of the pectoralis minor muscle as well as digital anomalies such as syndactyly. Other anomalies include the absence or hypoplasia of the ipsilateral breast, excavatum deformities, and rib aplasia. Defects are overwhelmingly unilateral and more commonly affect the right side. Bilateral manifestations, although exceedingly rare, have also been described. Many variations of the syndrome have been described, from mild hypoplasia of the pectoralis major musculature to severe hypoplasia of the thoracic wall. Upper limb defects also vary from mild syndactyly to severe brachysyndactyly.
