# Stereognosis

**Authors:** Tyler Schermann, Prasanna Tadi

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Stereognosis is the ability to identify the shape and form of a three-dimensional object and, therefore, its identity with tactile manipulation of that object in the absence of visual and auditory stimuli. The etymology of the word stereognosis is from the Greek for "stereo," meaning solid, and "gnosis," meaning knowledge. A distinction is necessary between manual and oral stereognosis, which occurs in the hands and mouth.
