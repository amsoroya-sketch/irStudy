# Median

**Authors:** Steven Tenny, Mary R. Hoffman

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

The median is the middle value in a set of numbers and is equivalent to the 50th percentile. In other words, the median is the midpoint of a set of numbers, with half of the values less than the median and half above it.
