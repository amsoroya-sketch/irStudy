# Segond Fracture

**Authors:** Edward J. Skinner, Donald D. Davis, Matthew A. Varacallo

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Segond fractures are defined as an avulsion type fracture of the lateral proximal tibia at the attachment of the anterolateral ligament. They are especially important because they are pathognomonic of a more complex knee injury, an anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) rupture. Segond fractures confirmed on plain radiographs necessitate further evaluation and investigation to disprove concomitant knee injuries, as Segond fractures are almost never isolated injuries. The one exception to this is in the pediatric population, where Segond fractures are rarely noted to be isolated injuries. This is, however, not the norm, and even children should undergo thorough evaluation and investigation for knee ligamentous injury.
