# Anatomy, Head and Neck: Blood Thymus Barrier

**Authors:** David M. Bruss, Susan Ely

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

The thymus is a bilobed lymphoid organ found in the anterosuperior mediastinum that plays a vital role in cellular immunity by facilitating the development of T lymphocytes. The thymus is anterior to the pulmonary outflow tract and proximal ascending aorta.
