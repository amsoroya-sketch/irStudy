# Neuroanatomy, Cranial Nerve 6 (Abducens)

**Authors:** Van Nguyen, Vamsi Reddy, Matthew A. Varacallo

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Cranial nerve six (CN VI), also known as the abducens nerve, is 1 of the nerves responsible for the extraocular motor functions of the eye, along with the oculomotor nerve (CN III) and the trochlear nerve (CN IV). See Image. The Cranial Nerves.
