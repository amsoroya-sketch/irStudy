# The National Practitioner Data Bank

**Authors:** Harrison R. Wermuth, Shane R. Sergent

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

The National Practitioner Data Bank (NPDB) is a centralized database overseen by the United States Department of Health and Human Services that collects reports of medical malpractice and other adverse actions taken against health care workers. Information is submitted to NPDB by agencies such as hospitals, state licensing boards, and medical malpractice insurance companies. The records the NPDB compiles include healthcare-related criminal convictions and civil judgments (eg, medical malpractice), final decisions rendered in licensure and certification actions, and other adjudicated actions. Information in the database is used to make employment decisions, track a physician's records, and support medical privileging. The records in NPDB are protected, and only authorized users (ie, hospitals or state licensure boards) can obtain a physician's record via a query.
