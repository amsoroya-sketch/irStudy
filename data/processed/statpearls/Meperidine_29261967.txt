# Meperidine

**Authors:** Rama Yasaei, Alan Rosani, Abdolreza Saadabadi

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Meperidine is a synthetic opioid in the phenylpiperidine class, primarily used for the treatment of moderate-to-severe pain. This medication is available in multiple forms, including intramuscular, subcutaneous, intravenous injections, syrup, and tablets. Historically favored in the 20th century for managing acute pain and some chronic pain cases, meperidine is also employed as an adjunct to preoperative medications to reduce shivering. This opioid exerts its effects primarily through agonism of the mu-opioid receptors, offering analgesic benefits while presenting a unique pharmacokinetic profile.
