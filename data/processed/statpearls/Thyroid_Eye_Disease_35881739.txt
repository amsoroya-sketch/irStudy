# Thyroid Eye Disease

**Authors:** Shalin S. Shah, Thomas J. Stokkermans, Bhupendra C. Patel

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Thyroid eye disease (TED) is a chronic, immune-mediated inflammation of the orbit and is the most common cause of unilateral and bilateral proptosis in adults. About 25% to 50% of Graves disease cases present with thyroid eye disease. Goiter-associated ophthalmopathy was first described in AD 1000 by Avicenna and Al-Jurjani. In 1835, Robert Graves, an Irish physician, described 4 young women with thyrotoxicosis, palpitations, thyroid gland enlargement, and exophthalmos. Graves ophthalmopathy is an orbital inflammation or infiltration involving the periocular soft tissues, resulting in proptosis and ophthalmoplegia.
