# Mandible Body Fracture

**Authors:** Prachi Jain, Manu Rathee

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Fracture of the mandible accounts for approximately 25% of maxillofacial fractures. Among the different mandibular fractures, the fracture of the body of the mandible accounts for almost 11% to 36%, with personal violence as the principal factor.
