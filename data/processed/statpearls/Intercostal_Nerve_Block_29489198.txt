# Intercostal Nerve Block

**Authors:** Anterpreet Dua, Brian M. Fitzgerald

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Intercostal nerve blocks are straightforward, versatile procedures that provide targeted analgesia for chest wall and upper abdominal pain, serving either as the primary pain management strategy or as an adjunct to multimodal therapy. These blocks interrupt nociceptive transmission along the intercostal nerves, reducing reliance on systemic opioids and enhancing patient comfort in both perioperative and trauma settings.
