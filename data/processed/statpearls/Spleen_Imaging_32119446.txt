# Spleen Imaging

**Authors:** William Coffey, Rashmi Balasubramanya

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

The spleen is a site of various pathologic processes, including congenital, infectious, traumatic, vascular, and neoplastic. Multiple modalities help image the spleen.
