# Flow Cytometry Blood Cell Identification

**Authors:** Pooja S. Kala, Muhammad Zubair

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Flow cytometry is a technique used to measure the physical and chemical properties of cells by analyzing their light scattering and fluorescence emission characteristics. The term flow cytometry refers to the measurement of a cell in a flowing sample. This technology allows for the simultaneous assessment of multiple parameters on individual cells, making it valuable for immunophenotyping cells from various sources, including blood, bone marrow, body fluids, fine-needle aspirates, and cell suspensions from freshly biopsied specimens. Flow cytometry can analyze approximately 30,000 cells per second. Each cell is analyzed for visible light scatter and one or multiple fluorescence parameters. This rapid, cost-effective, and widely available tool is used in diverse medical applications.
