# Ziconotide

**Authors:** Christopher S. Wie, Armen Derian

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Ziconotide is an intrathecal analgesic approved by the FDA for chronic severe pain in patients who are intolerant or refractory to systemic analgesics or intrathecal morphine. This medication demonstrates efficacy in managing intractable, severe chronic pain associated with both malignant and non-malignant conditions. By targeting specific calcium channels, ziconotide provides a unique alternative for patients with refractory pain syndromes. Clinical use requires careful consideration of contraindications, adverse events, toxicity, and drug interactions to ensure safe and effective therapy.
