# EMS Air Medical Transport

**Authors:** Timothy C. Steenhoff, Daniyal I. Siddiqui, Stephen F. Zohn

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Air medical transport includes fixed wing (airplanes) and rotor wing (helicopters) aircraft. They are utilized in situations for transporting patients where ground transportation (ambulances) are less effective. For example, helicopters are often used to transport critical trauma patients since time is an important factor in improving chances of survival. Airplanes are essential for carrying patients between states and countries. However, many factors have to be taken into consideration when choosing to utilize air medical transport.
