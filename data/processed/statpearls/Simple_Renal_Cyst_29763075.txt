# Simple Renal Cyst

**Authors:** Karen Garfield, Stephen W. Leslie

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

A simple kidney cyst is a pocket of fluid that originates from the surface of the kidney and is contained by a thin wall. One or more simple cysts may form within the kidneys. The simple cyst is the most common type of renal cyst.
