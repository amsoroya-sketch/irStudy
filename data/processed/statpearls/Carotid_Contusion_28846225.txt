# Carotid Contusion

**Authors:** Abdul Waheed, Mohammed M. Kassem, Lorena Gonzalez

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

The neck has several important anatomical structures, including the bilateral carotid arteries. Although carotid artery injury is uncommon, it typically occurs alongside other injuries due to the proximity of vital structures in the neck. Injury to the artery may be due to either penetrating or blunt trauma. When the trauma is penetrating, exsanguination and hematoma formation can quickly compromise the airway; when the carotid artery is injured bluntly, the sudden trauma usually initiates an intimal dissection or a hematoma formation. Carotid artery injuries caused by blunt trauma often cause thrombosis with delayed neurologic deficits and are associated with mortality rates up to 40%.
