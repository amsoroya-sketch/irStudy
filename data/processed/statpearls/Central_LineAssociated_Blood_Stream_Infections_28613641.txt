# Central Line–Associated Blood Stream Infections

**Authors:** Yazan Haddadin, Pavan Annamaraju, Hariharan Regunath

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

A central line-associated bloodstream infection (CLABSI) is defined as the recovery of a pathogen from a blood culture in a patient who had a central line at the time of infection or within 48 hours before the development of infection. Of all the healthcare-associated infections, CLABSIs are associated with a high-cost burden, accounting for approximately $46,000 per case. Most cases are preventable with proper aseptic techniques, surveillance, and management strategies.
