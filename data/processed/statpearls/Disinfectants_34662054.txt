# Disinfectants

**Authors:** Shahrzad Torabi, Farah Zahra

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Disinfectants and antiseptics are active chemical compounds known as biocides, which combat microorganisms on nonliving surfaces and human skin. Biocides such as alcohol, iodine, and chlorine have been used for many years. This activity describes some of the most commonly used disinfectants in hospitals and homes and their effectiveness.
