# Neuroanatomy, Broca Area

**Authors:** Taylor J. Stinnett, Vamsi Reddy, Matthew K. Zabel

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Processing and producing language is a complex process, with several structures within the brain all playing a vital role. The French physician and anatomist, Pierre Paul Broca, may have discovered the most crucial part when he identified a common region in the brain in two of his speech-impaired patients; this came to be known as the Broca (Broca's) area. This region, located in the posterior inferior frontal gyrus of the dominant hemisphere at Brodmann areas 44 (pars opercularis) and 45 (pars triangularis), is vital for language. More recent research includes other areas of the frontal lobe along with the Broca area as the Broca region.
