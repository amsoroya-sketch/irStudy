# Aflibercept

**Authors:** Basil Suresh, Preeti Patel

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Aflibercept is an anti-vascular endothelial growth factor (VEGF) medication approved for the treatment and management of neovascular (wet) age-related macular degeneration (nAMD), diabetic macular edema, macular edema secondary to retinal vein occlusion, diabetic retinopathy, and retinopathy of prematurity. Aflibercept is a humanized recombinant fusion protein that functions as a soluble decoy for VEGF receptors, also known as "VEGF trap," which is designed to inhibit the predominant signaling pathways mediating pathological angiogenesis and vascular permeability. The drug demonstrates high-affinity binding to VEGF-A, VEGF-B, and placental growth factor, with sufficient retinal penetration to achieve effective and sustained VEGF inhibition. Aflibercept and other anti-VEGF agents have become the gold standard in the management of nAMD. Ongoing clinical research continues to explore the therapeutic potential of aflibercept in a broader range of exudative retinal diseases, with approved indications steadily expanding.
