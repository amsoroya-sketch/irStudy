# Laryngeal Fracture

**Authors:** Sabina Rai, Fatima Anjum

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

The larynx is a well-protected structure surrounded by mandible, sternum, and cervical spine. Its elasticity and mobility add to its protection. Laryngeal cartilages do not ossify before 20 years of age, but thereafter, the larynx can vary among individuals regardless of age. The larynx has an important function in respiration, swallowing, and phonation. Minor trauma to larynx can disrupt these functions The significant impact of the injury on larynx can cause fracture and swelling leading to airway compromise.
