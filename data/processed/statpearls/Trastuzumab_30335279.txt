# Trastuzumab

**Authors:** Karl Greenblatt, Karam Khaddour

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Trastuzumab is an FDA-approved antineoplastic biologic that targets HER2-positive breast and gastric cancer. This activity outlines trastuzumab's various indications and off-label uses, including its combination with anthracycline or taxane-based chemotherapy for breast cancer, as monotherapy or in combination with paclitaxel for metastatic breast cancer, and in combination with cisplatin-based chemotherapy for gastric cancer. This discussion also includes the mechanism of action, administration methods, significant adverse effects, FDA-issued box warnings, toxicity profile, and monitoring requirements of trastuzumab. Understanding these elements is crucial for tailoring therapy to meet patient needs, optimizing dosing regimens, and minimizing adverse reactions.
