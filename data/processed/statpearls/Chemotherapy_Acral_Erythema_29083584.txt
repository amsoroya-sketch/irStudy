# Chemotherapy Acral Erythema

**Authors:** Anusha Chidharla, Tejaswi Kanderi, Anup Kasi

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Acral erythema, also known as Palmar-plantar erythrodysesthesia (PPE), palmoplantar erythrodysesthesia, toxic erythema of the palms and soles, hand-foot syndrome (HFS), or Burgdorf reaction. Many classic chemotherapeutic agents and newer molecular-targeted therapies cause this adverse event. It is characterized by intense, painful erythema of the palms and soles that can progress to the formation of vesicles or bullae. See Image. Chemotherapy Acral Erythema.
