# Hypoxic Brain Injury

**Authors:** Myriam Lacerte, Angela Hays Shapshak, Fassil B. Mesfin

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

The brain consumes much energy compared to its weight and size. It is highly metabolically active and exquisitely sensitive to hypoxia and hypoperfusion. Cellular injury can begin within minutes, and permanent brain injury follows if prompt intervention does not occur. Therefore, it is critical to understand the clinical presentation, pathophysiology, and management options.
