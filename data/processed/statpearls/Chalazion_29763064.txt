# Chalazion

**Authors:** Gary A. Jordan, Kevin Beier

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

A chalazion is a chronic sterile lipogranuloma. They are typically slowly enlarging and non-tender. A deep chalazion is caused by inflammation of a tarsal meibomian gland. A superficial chalazion is caused by inflammation of a Zeis gland. Chalazia are typically benign and self-limiting, though they can develop chronic complications. Recurrent chalazia should be evaluated for malignancy.
