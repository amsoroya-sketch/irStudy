# Acute Mountain Sickness Score

**Authors:** Amrit Ahluwalia, Philipp J. Underwood

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Acute mountain sickness (AMS) is a syndrome that arises in non-acclimatized individuals who ascend to high altitudes. It is a form of acute altitude illness that occurs as the atmospheric partial pressure of oxygen decreases with increasing altitude, inducing hypoxia. This condition typically occurs at altitudes >2500 meters; however, it can occur at lower elevations in high-risk individuals.
