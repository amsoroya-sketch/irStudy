# Macrocytic Anemia

**Authors:** Robert B. Killeen, Abdullah Adil

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Macrocytic anemia refers to macrocytosis, a mean corpuscular volume (MCV) >100 fL, in the setting of anemia. Generally, the following thresholds define anemia: For nonpregnant females: hemoglobin <12 g/dL or hematocrit (Hct) <36%. For pregnant females: hemoglobin <11 g/dL. For males: hemoglobin <13 g/dL or Hct <41% . Macrocytic anemia is divided into 2 forms: megaloblastic (hypersegmented neutrophils) and nonmegaloblastic. The megaloblastic form is due to impaired DNA synthesis from folate or vitamin B12 deficiencies, while the nonmegaloblastic moiety occurs from multiple mechanisms.
