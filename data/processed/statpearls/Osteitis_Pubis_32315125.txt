# Osteitis Pubis

**Authors:** Michael Dirkx, Christopher Vitale

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Osteitis pubis (OP) is a non-infectious, chronic inflammatory state of the pubic symphysis and surrounding soft tissues with multiple etiologies. It can result in groin or lower abdominal pain.
