# Double Orifice Mitral Valve

**Authors:** Rishard Abdul, Mohamed H. Alahmadi, Yuvraj S. Chowdhury

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Double-orifice mitral valve mitral valve, a rare congenital or acquired anomaly, is characterized by a mitral valve with a single fibrous annulus resulting in 2 orifices. First described by British pathologist William Smith Greenfield in 1876, literature on double-orifice mitral valves has been limited to case reports and small necropsy-based case series. Typically asymptomatic, the anomaly allows for normal blood flow between the 2 chambers of the left heart; however, symptoms may arise due to the development of mitral valve dysfunction, such as stenosis or regurgitation.
