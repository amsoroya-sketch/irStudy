# Posttraumatic Headache

**Authors:** Joseph Tessler, Lawrence J. Horn

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Post-traumatic headaches (PTHA) are a common sequela of traumatic brain injury (TBI) and may progress to chronic and possibly debilitating conditions. PTHA can be further subdivided into 2 general categories. Acute PTHA is attributed to TBI that resolves within 3 months, and persistent PTHA has not resolved within 3 months. There are several potentially overlapping phenotypes of PTHA.
