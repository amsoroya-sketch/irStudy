# Topical Steroid Withdrawal (Red Skin Syndrome)

**Authors:** Alpana Mohta, Nishad C. Sathe

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Topical corticosteroid withdrawal, also known as red skin syndrome or topical steroid addiction, is a drug-related dermatosis characterized by burning, itching, and erythema following the sudden termination of medium- to high-potency topical corticosteroids after prolonged use (typically at least after 3 months of use). The syndrome is a form of tachyphylaxis and rebound vasodilation with skin barrier dysfunction. Topical corticosteroid withdrawal most frequently affects the face and scrotum. However, every part of the body can be involved.
