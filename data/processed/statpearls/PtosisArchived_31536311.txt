# Ptosis(Archived)

**Authors:** Babar Shahzad, Marco A. Siccardi

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Ptosis is an abnormally low-positioned upper eyelid, also called blepharoptosis, which can decrease or even occlude vision completely. It may be congenital or acquired in origin. Proper management requires recognizing the exact etiology and treating it accordingly, whether surgically or medically, to improve patient outcome.
