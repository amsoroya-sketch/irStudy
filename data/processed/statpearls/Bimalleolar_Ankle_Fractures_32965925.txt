# Bimalleolar Ankle Fractures

**Authors:** Khaled Koujan, Ahmed Y. Saber

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Bimalleolar fractures are a type of ankle fracture that involves both the lateral and medial malleoli at the distal ends of the fibula and tibia, respectively. These 2 bones articulate with the talus to form the ankle or tibiotalar joint. The ankle is supported by ligaments on both the medial and lateral sides that stabilize the foot under the leg and lock the fibula and tibia together. Bimalleolar fractures can affect these ligaments, too. Damage to the ligaments, instead of a fracture of 1 of the malleoli, still produces an unstable fracture that likely needs surgical intervention.
