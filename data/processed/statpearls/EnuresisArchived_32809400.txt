# Enuresis(Archived)

**Authors:** Marcia Wilson, Vikas Gupta

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Enuresis is classified as an elimination disorder. It is frequently diagnosed in children who wet the bed or fail to establish continence of urine. It is a common disorder frequently seen in outpatient pediatric or urology clinics.
