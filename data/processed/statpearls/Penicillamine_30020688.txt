# Penicillamine

**Authors:** Stephanie G. Mejias, Preeti Patel, Kamleshun Ramphul

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Penicillamine is an FDA-approved agent indicated for Wilson disease and cystinuria and is also classified as a conventional disease-modifying antirheumatic drug (DMARD). Off-label, penicillamine has been administered to treat pediatric lead poisoning. This activity provides healthcare professionals with essential clinical information regarding the appropriate use of penicillamine in Wilson disease, rheumatoid arthritis, and cystinuria. The pharmacological profile of penicillamine, including its mechanism of action and considerations for adverse effects, is reviewed in detail.
