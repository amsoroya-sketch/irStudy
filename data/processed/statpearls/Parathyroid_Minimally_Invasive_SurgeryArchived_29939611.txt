# Parathyroid Minimally Invasive Surgery(Archived)

**Authors:** Samantha A. Wolfe, Abbey Fingeret

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Historically, a bilateral exploration was performed for the treatment of hyperparathyroidism. However, in 85% of cases of hyperparathyroidism, a single adenoma is the cause. With the advent of increasingly accurate localization studies, a minimally invasive approach to parathyroid surgery is emerging as a standard of care.
