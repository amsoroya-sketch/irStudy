# Clopidogrel

**Authors:** Craig J. Beavers, Preeti Patel, Imama A. Naqvi

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Clopidogrel is an antiplatelet medication approved for managing unstable angina, non-ST-segment elevation myocardial infarction (NSTEMI), and ST-segment elevation myocardial infarction (STEMI) in patients receiving fibrinolytic therapy. Indications also include secondary prevention in cases of recent myocardial infarction, stroke, and peripheral arterial disease. Several additional FDA-approved indications expand its use in clinical practice.
