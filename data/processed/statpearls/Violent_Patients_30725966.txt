# Violent Patients

**Authors:** Kristine Song, Robert B. Blankenship, Derek J. Schaller

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Addressing violent behavior in healthcare settings remains a critical challenge worldwide. Violence may stem from mental health disorders, substance use, emotional distress, or neurologic conditions. Patients requiring care cannot be denied treatment, even when exhibiting uncontrolled behavior, placing healthcare professionals in ethically complex situations. Violence disrupts care delivery, heightens stress, reduces productivity, and compromises patient safety. Identifying underlying causes and implementing effective prevention and intervention strategies are essential for maintaining a secure and supportive clinical environment.
