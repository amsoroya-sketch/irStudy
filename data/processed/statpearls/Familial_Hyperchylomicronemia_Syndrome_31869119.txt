# Familial Hyperchylomicronemia Syndrome

**Authors:** Manjari Regmi, Anis Rehman

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Familial hyperchylomicronemia syndrome (FCS) is a rare autosomal recessive metabolic disorder caused by mutations in lipoprotein lipase. It presents as elevated triglycerides and chylomicrons in the plasma that can cause an array of symptoms. Elevated triglycerides can cause a variety of symptoms, including serious episodes of acute pancreatitis.
