# ABO Blood Group System

**Authors:** Eleny C. Romanos-Sirakis, Diana Desai

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

The ABO blood group system was first discovered in the early 1900s, and our understanding of it has significantly increased since then. These advancements have contributed to safer blood transfusion practices. Beyond transfusion medicine, the ABO system has also found applications in population studies by anthropologists, forensic investigations by law enforcement, and paternity cases in legal settings.
