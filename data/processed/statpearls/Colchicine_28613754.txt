# Colchicine

**Authors:** Nazia M. Sadiq, Kenneth J. Robinson, Jamie M. Terrell

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Colchicine is FDA-approved for gout prevention, treatment of acute gouty flares, and familial Mediterranean fever. Off-label applications include hepatic cirrhosis, primary biliary cirrhosis, and pseudogout. The primary mechanism of action involves anti-inflammatory effects, which are central to managing these conditions. Understanding the pharmacodynamics, dosing, adverse event profile, and toxicity is essential for optimizing patient outcomes.
