# Ventriculitis

**Authors:** Walter A. Hall, Sunil Munakomi

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Ventriculitis is the inflammation of the ependymal lining of the cerebral ventricles secondary to an infectious process. Other terms used to describe ventriculitis are "ependymitis," "ventricular empyema," "pyocephalus," and "pyogenic ventriculitis." This indolent but potentially lethal infection can arise after incomplete or unsuccessful treatment of meningitis. Early diagnosis is essential for determining an appropriate and effective treatment modality. Ventriculitis is of particular concern in patients with temporary external ventricular drains (EVDs) or permanent intraventricular shunts that enable cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) diversion.
