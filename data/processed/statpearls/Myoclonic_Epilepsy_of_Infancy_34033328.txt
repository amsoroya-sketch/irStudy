# Myoclonic Epilepsy of Infancy

**Authors:** Palanikumar Balasundaram, Arayamparambil C. Anilkumar

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Myoclonic epilepsy of infancy (MEI) is a rare self-limited epileptic syndrome characterized by brief myoclonic seizures in previously healthy and developmentally normal children with onset in the first 3 years of life. MEI is also known as benign myoclonic epilepsy in infants (BMEI), a term first used by Dravet and Bureau in a 1981 study. Before this study, several similar cases had been reported but with varying terminology, including "myoclonic epilepsy of childhood," which was used by Jeavons.  MEI does not have a benign course or prognosis in some children, so the word benign was removed from the name. The International League Against Epilepsy (ILAE) has included MEI among other neonatal and infantile epileptic syndromes under the classification of idiopathic generalized epilepsies with age-related onset. However, MEI can present as a generalized epilepsy in a child with a developmental encephalopathy or normal intellect.
