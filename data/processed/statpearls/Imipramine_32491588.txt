# Imipramine

**Authors:** Rola Fayez, Vikas Gupta

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Imipramine is a medication used in the treatment of depression and anxiety. It is a tertiary amine tricyclic antidepressant. This activity reviews the indications, contraindications, activity, adverse events, and other key elements of imipramine therapy in the clinical setting as continuous monitoring of the drug and the acute management of tricyclic overdose. These factors will be clinically relevant and essential for interprofessional team members in taking care of patients on imipramine therapy.
