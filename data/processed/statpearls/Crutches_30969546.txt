# Crutches

**Authors:** Warees M. Warees, Lisa Clayton, Matthew Slane

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Crutches are medical devices designed to aid in ambulation, by transferring body weight from the legs to the torso and arms.  They are mainly used to assist individuals with lower extremity injuries and/or neurological impairment.  Most crutches classify into the following three categories: axilla, forearm, and platform.
