# Managed Care Organization

**Authors:** Joseph Heaton, Prasanna Tadi

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Managed care organizations are integrated entities in the healthcare system, which endeavor to reduce healthcare expenditures costs. Since the 1970s, managed care organizations have shaped healthcare delivery in the United States through preventative medicine strategies, financial provisioning, and treatment guidelines.
