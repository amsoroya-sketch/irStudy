**Authors:** Akul Munjal, Stephen W. Leslie

## Abstract

Prostate cancer is one of the most commonly diagnosed cancers in men. Worldwide, it is the second most common male malignancy (lung cancer is first). In 2018, more than 1.6 million men were diagnosed with prostate cancer globally and over 366,000 died from it. The American Cancer Society has estimated that in 2021 there will be 248,530 new cases diagnosed in the U.S., with 34,130 deaths from the disease. The death rate from prostate cancer significantly dropped from the mid-1990s until 2014 at which point the death rate stabilized.
