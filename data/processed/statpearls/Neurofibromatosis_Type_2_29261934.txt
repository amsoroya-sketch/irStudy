**Authors:** Ruchi Tiwari, Achint K. Singh

## Abstract

Neurofibromatosis type 2 (NF2) is an autosomal dominant disorder characterized by the development of multiple tumors involving the central nervous system (CNS). See Image. Neurofibromatosis.
