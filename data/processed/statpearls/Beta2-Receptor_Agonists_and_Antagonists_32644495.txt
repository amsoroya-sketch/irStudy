# Beta2-Receptor Agonists and Antagonists

**Authors:** NourEldin R. Abosamak, Preeti Patel, Mohamed H. Shahin

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Beta-2 adrenergic receptors play a central role in the management of bronchospastic disorders and are among the most frequently targeted pathways in respiratory and cardiovascular care. This activity examines the molecular mechanisms of β2 receptor signaling and compares short-, long-, and ultra-long-acting agonists, as well as nonselective and cardioselective antagonists. This activity also reviews the indications, routes of administration, and evidence-based dosing strategies. Additionally, this activity identifies key adverse effects of β2 agonists and antagonists, and implements monitoring protocols for serum electrolytes, heart rate, and pulmonary function to ensure patient safety.
