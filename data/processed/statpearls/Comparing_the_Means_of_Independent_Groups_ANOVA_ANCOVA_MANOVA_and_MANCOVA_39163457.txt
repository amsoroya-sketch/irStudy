# Comparing the Means of Independent Groups: ANOVA, ANCOVA, MANOVA, and MANCOVA

**Authors:** Nancy M. Buderer, Grace D. Brannan

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Testing the difference between the means of independent (unrelated) categorical groups involves parametric statistical tests. Comparing the means between 2 independent groups requires a two-sample Student’s T-test (or unpaired or independent samples t-tests). This article focuses on statistical methods for testing the difference between the means of 3 or more independent categorical groups based on a continuous dependent variable. These methods require an Analysis of Variance (ANOVA) test or one of its variations.
