# Microspherophakia

**Authors:** Kirandeep Kaur, Bharat Gurnani

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Microspherophakia is a rare congenital anomaly characterized by the abnormal spherical shape of the crystalline lens. It is characterized by an increased anteroposterior thickness of the lens associated with reduced equatorial diameter. The primary pathology lies in the development of zonules. The condition is often bilateral and is characterized by small, thick, and spherical lenses. Mutation in the LTBP2 gene is often implicated as the underlying pathology. The most common associations are lens subluxation, anterior or posterior dislocation, high myopia, accommodation deficits, or raised intraocular pressures. Rare cases of microspherophakia associated with retinitis pigmentosa, Axenfeld Rieger anomaly, XYY syndrome have also been reported.
