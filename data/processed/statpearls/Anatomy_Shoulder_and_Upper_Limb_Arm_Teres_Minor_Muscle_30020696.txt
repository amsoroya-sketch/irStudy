# Anatomy, Shoulder and Upper Limb, Arm Teres Minor Muscle

**Authors:** Pallavi Juneja, John B. Hubbard

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

The teres minor is a narrow, intrinsic shoulder muscle that extends from the lateral border of the scapula to the greater tubercle (or tuberosity) of the humerus. It contributes to the “rotator cuff,” a capsule of muscles and tendons that collectively stabilize the glenohumeral joint. The teres minor is responsible for lateral, or external, rotation of the arm at the shoulder.
