# Hemolacria

**Authors:** Koushik Tripathy, Baby Salini

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Hemolacria or bloody epiphora is the presence of blood in the tear. Alternative names of the condition include bloody tears, blood-stained tears, dacryohemorrhea, hematodacryorrhea, hemolacrimia, sanguineous tears, sanguineous lacrimation, hematic epiphora, dacryohemorrhysis, lacrimae cruentae, and tears of blood.
