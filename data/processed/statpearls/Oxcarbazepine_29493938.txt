# Oxcarbazepine

**Authors:** Charles V. Preuss, Gagandeep Randhawa, Abdolreza Saadabadi

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Oxcarbazepine is an anticonvulsant used to treat partial seizures. As a 10-keto derivative of carbamazepine, oxcarbazepine exhibits notable pharmacological differences from its parent compound, including reduced hepatic enzyme induction and an altered metabolism profile. These distinctions contribute to unique therapeutic effects and safety considerations. Approved for use in 2000, oxcarbazepine has since become a widely utilized agent in seizure management due to favorable efficacy and tolerability in many patient populations.
