# Succinylcholine Chloride

**Authors:** Heather H. Hager, Preeti Patel, Bracken Burns

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Succinylcholine is a short-acting depolarizing neuromuscular blocking agent approved by the FDA for co-administration with sedatives or hypnotics. By blocking acetylcholine, succinylcholine disrupts cholinergic receptors in the parasympathetic and sympathetic nervous systems, leading to skeletal muscle relaxation. This mechanism facilitates rapid endotracheal intubation, surgical procedures, and mechanical ventilation. Off-label use includes adjunctive therapy during electroconvulsive therapy to control electrically induced muscle contractions.
