# Pearly Penile Papule

**Authors:** Lauren W. Love, Talel Badri, Michael L. Ramsey

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Pearly penile papules, also known as papillomatosis corona penis, corona capillitii, hirsuties coronae glandis, papillae coronis glandis, and hirsutoid papillomas, are benign lesions of the penis.
