# Budd-Chiari Syndrome

**Authors:** Asif A. Hitawala, Eric Goosenberg

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Budd-Chiari syndrome is an uncommon disorder defined as hepatic venous outflow obstruction, thrombotic or otherwise, not due to pericardial disease, cardiac disease, or sinusoidal obstruction syndrome (hepatic veno-occlusive disease). Primary Budd-Chiari syndrome is a particularly rare (diagnosed in 1 person/1,000,000 persons/year) and highly fatal condition in which obstruction is predominantly due to thrombosis or phlebitis, diseases of the wall of the hepatic vein. In contrast, secondary Budd-Chiari syndrome is defined as compression or invasion of the hepatic veins or the inferior vena cava by a lesion that originates outside the vein (eg, malignancy).
