# Topical Corticosteroids

**Authors:** Sarah Gabros, Trevor A. Nessel, Patrick M. Zito

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Topical corticosteroids serve as a cornerstone in managing inflammatory and pruritic dermatologic conditions. These medications are FDA-approved for hyperproliferation, immune dysregulation, and inflammatory responses. This activity reviews the indications, mechanism of action, contraindications, and key considerations for administration and monitoring. The adverse event profile, potential toxicity, and relevant drug interactions are also addressed to support informed clinical decision-making.
