# Open Airway Procedural Sedation(Archived)

**Authors:** Paul Chang, Thomas Elias

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Several professional organizations have made numerous attempts to define open airway procedural sedation (OAPS). Although minor differences can be seen among these definitions, their common elements can be utilized to formulate a comprehensive definition of OAPS, which can be summarized as the facilitation of diagnostic and therapeutic procedures by providing anxiolysis, analgesia, akinesia, or amnesia through the administration of 1 or more pharmacological agents to maintain airway patency and protective reflexes, spontaneous breathing, as well as hemodynamic stability.  Substantial variability exists in the practice of OAPS, which is performed by practitioners with varying backgrounds and skill levels in various clinical settings. It is important to review core principles that remain unchanged irrespective of these variables.
