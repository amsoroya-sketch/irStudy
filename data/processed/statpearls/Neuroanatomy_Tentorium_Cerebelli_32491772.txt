# Neuroanatomy, Tentorium Cerebelli

**Authors:** Michael I. Ita, Bruno Bordoni

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

The tentorium cerebelli is one of four distinguishable dural reflections which partition the cranial cavity into incomplete compartments. The falx cerebri, diaphragma sellae, and falx cerebelli constitute the other three recognizable dural folds. The French anatomist Jacques Bénigne Winslow in 1732 was the first to use the designation 'La Tente' in describing the tentorium cerebelli, which divides the cranial cavity into supratentorial and infratentorial compartments.
