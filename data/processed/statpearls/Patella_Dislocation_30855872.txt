# Patella Dislocation

**Authors:** Zara Hayat, Youssef El Bitar, Justin L. Case

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Patellar instability is a spectrum of conditions ranging from intermittent subluxations to dislocation. Patients typically present with obvious deformity and an inability to extend the knee. The recurrence rate following a first-time dislocation is around 15-60%. Generalized patellar instability is thought to represent up to 3% of clinical presentations involving the knee. In most cases, it can be managed conservatively with physiotherapy and bracing, except in the presence of a fracture or recurrent episodes.
