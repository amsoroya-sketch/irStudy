# Coxiella burnetii Infection(Archived)

**Authors:** Karun Neupane, Daniel Kaswan

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Coxiella burnetii is the causative agent of Q fever. Q fever is a zoonotic disease primarily affecting individuals who work with farm animals. While most cases remain asymptomatic, symptomatic patients most commonly develop a febrile illness. Effective treatment and vaccines are available for this condition. However, if left untreated, it can develop into a chronic infection that affects multiple organs, including the heart, bones, and lungs. The workers in the animal industry should be educated about the risk of contracting this disease and the appropriate measures for prevention.
