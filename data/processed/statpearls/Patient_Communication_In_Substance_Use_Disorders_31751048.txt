# Patient Communication In Substance Use Disorders

**Authors:** Kevin Wu, Jeff Baker

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Patients with alcohol or other substance use disorders often have complex presentations of addictive behaviors and medical comorbidities, making these patients uniquely challenging to treat. Because of the complex nature of these cases, communication failures can lead to missed therapeutic opportunities. Given the shift in healthcare to a more patient-centered approach and the positive association between high-quality communication with a healthcare provider and improved patient outcomes, it is essential to acknowledge and assess factors that generate dissonance between the patient and the clinician. Furthermore, providers can better manage interventions, referrals for treatment, and assessments of patient’s willingness to change with more effective communication tools.
