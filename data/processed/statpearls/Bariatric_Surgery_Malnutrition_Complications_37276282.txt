# Bariatric Surgery Malnutrition Complications

**Authors:** Venu Madhav Ganipisetti, Sowjanya Naha

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Bariatric surgery is an effective treatment that produces significant and sustained improvement in metabolic disorders and comorbidities associated with obesity. However, bariatric surgery is associated with an increased risk of malnutrition post-operatively. Moreover, bariatric surgery can exacerbate preexisting nutritional deficiencies in patients with obesity who undergo such procedures. Patients can also be lost to follow-up after surgery and fail to adhere to dietary recommendations necessary to prevent such deficiencies.  With the increasing popularity of bariatric surgery as a treatment for obesity, it is crucial to be aware of such complications and recognize the presenting symptoms of nutritional deficiencies so that necessary measures can be taken for prevention and treatment.
