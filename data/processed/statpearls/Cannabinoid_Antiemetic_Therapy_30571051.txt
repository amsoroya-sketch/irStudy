# Cannabinoid Antiemetic Therapy

**Authors:** Preeti Patel, Ryan S. Sauls

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Various cannabinoid-based antiemetic agents are approved by the Food and Drug Administration for specific indications, including the management of chemotherapy-induced nausea and vomiting after first-line treatments fail to address the condition adequately. Dronabinol and nabilone are synthetic forms of tetrahydrocannabinol that have demonstrated efficacy in reducing chemotherapy-induced nausea and vomiting and in treating anorexia associated with AIDS. One cannabidiol product has been approved by the Food and Drug Administration for seizure management in pediatric patients with Lennox-Gastaut and Dravet syndromes but is not approved for antiemetic use. This activity reviews the pharmacological properties, mechanisms of action, and clinical applications of cannabinoid antiemetics in current medical practice.
