# Motor Vehicle Collisions

**Authors:** Tammy J. Toney-Butler, Matthew A. Varacallo

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

According to the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), motor vehicle collisions (MVC) in 2013 accounted for over 2 million injuries and more than 32,000 deaths annually in the United States (US) . The National Spinal Cord Injury Statistical Center cited the most common cause of spinal cord injuries (SCI) in the US between the years of 2010 and 2013 was MVCs, accounting for 38% of these injuries.
