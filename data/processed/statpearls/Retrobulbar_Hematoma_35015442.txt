# Retrobulbar Hematoma

**Authors:** Shaina Kumar, Nancy Blace

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Retrobulbar hematoma (RBH) is a rare yet sight-threatening diagnosis. Although mild cases may occur, a devastating consequence is orbital compartment syndrome, which can potentially lead to complete vision loss if not treated promptly. The definitive management is lateral orbital canthotomy and cantholysis, which can be vision-saving (See Image. Retrobulbar Hematoma).
