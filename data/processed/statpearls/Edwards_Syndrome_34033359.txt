# Edwards Syndrome

**Authors:** Palanikumar Balasundaram, Indirapriya Darshini Avulakunta

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Edwards syndrome, also called trisomy 18, is an autosomal chromosomal disorder caused by an extra copy of chromosome 18. This disorder is one of the autosomal trisomy syndromes, second in frequency only to trisomy 21. Edwards syndrome was first reported by Edwards et al in 1960, who reported a neonate with multiple congenital malformations and cognitive deficits. Smith et al confirmed the extra copy of chromosome 18 as an underlying cause of Edwards syndrome.
