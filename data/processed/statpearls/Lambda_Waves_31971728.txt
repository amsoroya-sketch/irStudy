# Lambda Waves

**Authors:** Marco Cascella, Susanta Bandyopadhyay

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

The electroencephalographic (EEG) signal is a bioelectric potential related to brain activity recorded on the scalp with electrodes and appropriate instrumentation. The system for measuring brain bioelectric potentials has the function of capturing the weak electrical signal on the scalp, amplifying it, processing it (including digitization), and finally, registering it. Increasingly complex processing of the raw signal (eg, processed EEG through Fourier analysis or Joint Time-Frequency Analysis, JTFA, and dedicated algorithms) enables the use of the signal in various fields of application, such as general anesthesia and research purposes (eg, neuroscience and cognitive psychology).
