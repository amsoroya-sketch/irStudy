# Darier Disease

**Authors:** Shawn J. Schmieder, Nishad C. Sathe, Juan A. Rosario-Collazo

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Darier disease, previously known as keratosis follicularis, is a genodermatosis inherited in an autosomal dominant fashion. This rare disease classically features multiple red or brown papules with hyperkeratosis, nail abnormalities (eg, longitudinal erythronychia), and mucosal changes that present initially around puberty and are exacerbated throughout the lifespan, usually with external exposures, such as sunlight. The condition is found worldwide and classified with other acantholytic disorders, such as Hailey-Hailey disease and Grover disease.
