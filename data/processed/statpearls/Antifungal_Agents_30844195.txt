# Antifungal Agents

**Authors:** Preeti Patel, Patrick M. Zito

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Fungi are eukaryotic organisms found in nearly every environment, with only a limited subset contributing to human disease. These pathogenic fungi can cause infections ranging from mild cutaneous conditions to invasive, life-threatening diseases such as cryptococcal meningitis. Antifungal agents serve as a critical therapeutic class for managing these infections. Understanding the pharmacologic characteristics of antifungal medications—including spectrum of activity, mechanism of action, and pharmacokinetics—is essential for effective treatment selection.
