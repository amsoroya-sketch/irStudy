# Atypical Fibroxanthoma

**Authors:** Logan Kolb, George J. Schmieder

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Atypical fibroxanthoma is a rare, low-grade superficial sarcoma frequently presenting as a red nodule or plaque. This condition is a superficial variant of undifferentiated pleomorphic sarcoma (formerly malignant fibrous histiocytoma). Although atypical fibroxanthoma has similar histologic features to undifferentiated pleomorphic sarcoma, it behaves less aggressively.
