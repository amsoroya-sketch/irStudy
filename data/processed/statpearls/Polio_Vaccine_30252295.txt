# Polio Vaccine

**Authors:** Meghan O'Grady, Paul J. Bruner

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Poliomyelitis is a highly infectious viral illness that can lead to permanent disability. Global vaccination efforts have drastically reduced the incidence of polio, bringing the disease to the brink of eradication. The oral polio vaccine remains a vital tool in many parts of the world due to its low cost and ease of administration, though there is a rare risk of paralysis associated with this vaccine. In contrast, the inactivated polio vaccine is used exclusively in countries with adequate sanitation infrastructure, including the United States, where the risk of vaccine-derived poliovirus is minimized.
