# Brexanolone

**Authors:** Yusra Azhar, Amad U. Din

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Recently, brexanolone has been discontinued by the manufacturer. See Federal Register Notice.  This topic remains for educational purposes on postpartum depression and is no longer recommended in clinical practice.
