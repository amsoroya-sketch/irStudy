# Anovulatory Bleeding

**Authors:** Katrina Jones, Sharon Sung

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Anovulatory bleeding, also known as abnormal uterine bleeding (AUB) associated with ovulatory dysfunction (AUB-O), is a type of noncyclic uterine bleeding characterized by irregular, prolonged, and often heavy menstruation. This condition is one of the recognized causes of AUB, which is a frequently encountered complaint in primary care, affecting up to one-third of women of childbearing age.  Although AUB-O is commonly observed during menarche and perimenopause, this condition can present at any stage of reproductive life. Diagnosing AUB-O requires excluding other structural and physiological causes of AUB, which should be ruled out through a thorough history, physical examination, and, when necessary, laboratory tests and imaging.
