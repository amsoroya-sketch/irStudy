# Biochemistry, Dissolution and Solubility

**Authors:** Jue Xi Lu, Connor Tupper, Alejandra V. Gutierrez, John Murray

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Dissolution is the process where a solute in a gaseous, liquid, or solid phase dissolves in a solvent to form a solution. Solubility is the maximum concentration of a solute that can dissolve in a solvent at a given temperature. At the maximum solute concentration, the solution is said to be saturated. The units of solubility can be provided in mol/L or g/L. Factors that affect solubility include the concentration of the solute, temperature of the system, pressure (for gases in solution), and polarity of the solute and the solvent.
