# Sphenopalatine Ganglion Block

**Authors:** Christopher E. Alexander, Anterpreet Dua

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

The role of the sphenopalatine ganglion (SPG) in the pathogenesis of pain and its use was first described as sphenopalatine neuralgia by Sluder in 1908. He described sphenopalatine neuralgia as a unilateral facial pain symptom complex with associated neuralgic, motor, sensory, and gustatory manifestations. Today, the blockade of the SPG is utilized to treat several painful conditions.
