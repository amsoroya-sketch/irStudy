# Overview on Ordering and Evaluation of Laboratory Tests

**Authors:** Muhammad Zubair, Husam El Sharu, Marjorie V. Launico

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

The laboratory medicine service in a hospital addresses relevant investigations of patients’ presenting complaints and, in some cases, the detection and prevention of disease. The process includes a clinical request for a specific investigation, specimen analysis, result interpretation, and timely reporting. These functions occur within a designated area of the hospital, the pathology laboratory, supervised by a physician specialized in pathology. The pathologist issues reports and guides physician colleagues on optimal utilization of laboratory services. Laboratory size and facilities depend on hospital bed capacity, the population served, and the diversity of cases encountered.
