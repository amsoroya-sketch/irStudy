# Pulmonary Eosinophilia

**Authors:** Moiz Salahuddin, Fatima Anjum, Sujith V. Cherian

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Pulmonary eosinophilia (PE) is defined as the infiltration of eosinophils into the lung compartments constituting airways, interstitium, and alveoli. Various infections, drugs, parasites, autoimmune processes, malignancies, and obstructive lung diseases have been associated with increased eosinophils in the lungs.
