# Rosuvastatin

**Authors:** Tushar Bajaj, Preeti Patel, Al O. Giwa

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Rosuvastatin is a lipid-lowering medication belonging to the statin class that inhibits 3-hydroxy-3-methylglutaryl-coenzyme A reductase, the rate-limiting enzyme in cholesterol biosynthesis. By decreasing hepatic cholesterol production and upregulating low-density lipoprotein receptors, rosuvastatin effectively reduces circulating low-density lipoprotein cholesterol and total cholesterol levels, thereby lowering the risk of atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease. The medication has demonstrated efficacy in both primary and secondary prevention of cardiovascular events, including myocardial infarction and stroke. Rosuvastatin is administered orally and is available in multiple dosage strengths to accommodate individualized treatment goals based on lipid profiles, comorbidities, and overall cardiovascular risk. Monitoring parameters include lipid panels, hepatic function, and creatine kinase levels in select cases. Adverse effects such as myalgia, elevated liver enzymes, and rare instances of rhabdomyolysis necessitate clinician vigilance. Rosuvastatin therapy remains a cornerstone in dyslipidemia management, contributing significantly to the reduction of cardiovascular morbidity and mortality.
