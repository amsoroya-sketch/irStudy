# Biochemistry, Guanylate Cyclase

**Authors:** Yasaman Pirahanchi, Manjari Dimri

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Guanylyl cyclase (also known as guanylate cyclase) is an enzyme that catalyzes the synthesis of cyclic guanosine 3′,5′-monophosphate (cGMP) from guanosine 5' triphosphate (GTP). Guanylyl cyclase exists in both a membrane-bound and soluble form in the cell. The membrane-bound form is a plasma membrane receptor, while soluble forms of guanylyl cyclase undergo activation by nitric oxide. Nitric oxide then functions as a primary messenger, amplifying the signal intracellularly. This review discusses guanylyl cyclase's biochemistry, function, and clinical relevance.
