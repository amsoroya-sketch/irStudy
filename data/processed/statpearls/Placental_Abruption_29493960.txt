# Placental Abruption

**Authors:** Maggie Sullivan, Christy L. Skelly, Caroline Rouse

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Placental abruption is the early separation of a placenta from the lining of the uterus before the completion of the second stage of labor. This condition is a leading cause of bleeding during the second half of pregnancy. Placental abruption is a relatively rare but serious complication of pregnancy that puts the well-being of both mother and fetus at risk. This condition is also known as abruptio placentae (see Image. Placental Abruption).
