# Tissue Plasminogen Activator Therapy

**Authors:** Preeti Patel, Pradeep C. Bollu

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Alteplase, a recombinant tissue plasminogen activator (tPA), is a potent thrombolytic agent approved by the US Food and Drug Administration for the treatment of acute ischemic stroke (within 3 hours of onset), ST-segment elevation myocardial infarction, massive pulmonary embolism with hemodynamic instability, and occluded IV catheters. Alteplase works by converting plasminogen to plasmin, promoting clot breakdown and restoring blood flow. Administration of alteplase is highly time-sensitive, particularly in ischemic stroke, and requires accurate weight-based dosing, careful patient selection, and close monitoring for complications such as bleeding.
