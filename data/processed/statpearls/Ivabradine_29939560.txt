# Ivabradine

**Authors:** Mirembe Reed, Preeti Patel

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Ivabradine is an important therapeutic option in the management of stable heart failure, particularly in patients with a left ventricular ejection fraction of 35% or less who remain tachycardic despite guideline-directed therapy. By selectively inhibiting the I(f) current in the sinoatrial node, ivabradine lowers heart rate without affecting myocardial contraction, relaxation, or ventricular repolarization. Because an elevated resting heart rate is strongly associated with adverse cardiovascular outcomes, the appropriate use of ivabradine plays a significant role in reducing hospitalizations related to worsening heart failure.
