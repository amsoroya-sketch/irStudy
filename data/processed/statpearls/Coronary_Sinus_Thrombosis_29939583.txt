# Coronary Sinus Thrombosis

**Authors:** Wajeed Masood, Kranthi K. Sitammagari

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

The coronary venous system generally receives minimal attention in the medical literature. Even though the coronary sinus is a major access site for many invasive cardiology procedures, healthcare professionals rarely encounter diseases related to the coronary venous system. The exception is congenital malformations. Coronary sinus thrombosis is a rare and severe condition that is usually a post-procedural complication following invasive right heart instrumentation. Sometimes, the condition occurs spontaneously.
