# Cicatricial Pemphigoid

**Authors:** Hasnain A. Syed, Matthew R. Hall

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Cicatricial pemphigoid represents a rare, chronic autoimmune blistering disorder characterized by the potential to cause scarring. The disease may affect the skin alone, the mucous membranes alone, or both. When confined to the mucous membranes, the condition is often termed mucous membrane pemphigoid. In cases limited to the ocular surfaces, the term ocular pemphigoid is commonly used. The likelihood of scarring depends largely on the anatomical site of disease involvement. Diagnosing cicatricial pemphigoid can present significant challenges, particularly in the early stages, when clinical features may be nonspecific. Given the potential for serious complications, eg, vision loss and airway obstruction, early recognition and prompt initiation of aggressive therapy may help prevent irreversible damage and improve long-term outcomes.
