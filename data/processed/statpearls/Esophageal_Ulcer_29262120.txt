# Esophageal Ulcer

**Authors:** Cecilia Nguyen, Chenlu Tian, Hrishikesh Samant

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Esophageal ulcers are breaks in the esophageal mucosa that can form at any point along the esophagus. Exposure to irritants causes inflammation of the esophageal mucosa, leading to erosion and the formation of open sores. If left untreated, these mucosal wounds can lead to complications, such as bleeding, stricture formation, and perforation. With treatment, esophageal ulcers can heal within a few weeks to months but can recur if the underlying etiology is not adequately managed.
