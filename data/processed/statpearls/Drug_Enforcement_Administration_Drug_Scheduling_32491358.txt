# Drug Enforcement Administration Drug Scheduling

**Authors:** Michael J. Lopez, Charles V. Preuss, Prasanna Tadi

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Drug scheduling became mandated under The Federal Comprehensive Drug Abuse Prevention and Control Act of 1970 (also known as the Controlled Substances Act). The law addresses controlled substances within Title II. Based upon this law, the United States Drug Enforcement Agency (DEA) maintains a list of controlled medications and illicit substances categorized from Schedule I to V.
