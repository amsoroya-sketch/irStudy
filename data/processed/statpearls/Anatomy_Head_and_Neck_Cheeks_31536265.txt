# Anatomy, Head and Neck, Cheeks

**Authors:** John D. Nguyen, Hieu Duong

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

The human face is made up of many muscles, vessels, glands, tissues, and organs. Among the most distinct features on the human face are the cheeks. To the naked eye, the cheeks appear only as a small part of the face.   The cheeks are described as the region below the eyes but above the jawline. The cheeks span between the nose and the ears. The cheeks are made up of many muscles, fat pads, glands, and tissues. This complex composition allows the checks to participate in eating, talking, and facial expression.
