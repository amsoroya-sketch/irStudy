# Inamrinone

**Authors:** Preeti Patel, Charles V. Preuss

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Inamrinone, also known as amrinone, is a drug that increases cardiac output through its positive inotropic and vasodilatory effects. It is prescribed for the short-term management of congestive heart failure, relieving symptoms of CHF such as fatigue, weakness, edema, dyspnea, orthopnea, and paroxysmal nocturnal dyspnea. There is no indication for or known benefit of long-term administration of inamrinone in the safe and effective management of congestive heart failure.
