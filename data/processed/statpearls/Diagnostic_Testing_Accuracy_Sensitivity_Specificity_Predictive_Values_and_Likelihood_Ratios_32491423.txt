# Diagnostic Testing Accuracy: Sensitivity, Specificity, Predictive Values and Likelihood Ratios

**Authors:** Jacob Shreffler, Martin R. Huecker

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

To make clinical decisions and guide patient care, providers must comprehend the likelihood of a patient having a disease, combining an understanding of pretest probability and diagnostic assessments. Diagnostic tools are routinely utilized in healthcare settings to determine treatment methods; however, many of these tools are subject to error.
