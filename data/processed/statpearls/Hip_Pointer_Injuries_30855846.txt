# Hip Pointer Injuries

**Authors:** Matthew A. Varacallo, Bruno Bordoni

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

A hip pointer is a contusion of the iliac crest and/or the greater trochanteric region of the femur following a direct impact or collision. The management of these injuries requires coordinated care between athletic trainers, clinical providers, primary care, and sports medicine specialists. The majority of these injuries improve with standard, first-line nonoperative management modalities.
