# Mycoplasma Pneumonia

**Authors:** Basma Abdulhadi, John Kiel

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Mycoplasma pneumonia is a bacteria that can infect humans. It usually causes upper respiratory tract infections but can also cause pneumonia, and it is one of the most common causes of atypical pneumonia in the United States. Many extrapulmonary infections have been attributed to M pneumoniae infections. However, a causal link is yet to be established. Reimann first described this condition after observing a series of 7 patients with marked constitutional symptoms and termed it 'primary atypical pneumonia.'
