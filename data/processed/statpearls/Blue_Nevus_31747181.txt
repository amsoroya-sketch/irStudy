# Blue Nevus

**Authors:** Steve S. Austad, Leela Athalye

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

The term "blue nevus" describes a group of skin lesions characterized by dermal proliferation of melanocytes presenting as blue to black nodules on the head, extremities, or buttocks. (see Image. Giant Blue Nevus). In most cases, they are acquired and present as a solitary lesion but may also be congenital and appear at multiple sites. In addition to multiple less-known variants, there are 2 widely known histological subtypes: common blue nevus (dendritic blue nevus or Jadassohn-Tieche type) and cellular blue nevus. Several variants of common nevi include epithelioid, sclerosing, amelanocytic, and combined. See Image. Blue Nevus on Thigh.
