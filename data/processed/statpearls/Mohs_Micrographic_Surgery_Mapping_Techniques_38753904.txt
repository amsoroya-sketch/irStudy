# Mohs Micrographic Surgery Mapping Techniques

**Authors:** Kevin M. Pennycook, Chris Buckley

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Mohs micrographic surgery stands at the forefront of dermatologic oncology, revolutionizing the treatment of cutaneous malignancies. With its meticulous microscopic margin control, Mohs surgery offers unparalleled precision in tumor removal, making it the gold standard when resecting a wide range of skin cancers. Acting as both surgeon and pathologist, the Mohs surgeon navigates the complexities of tumor extirpation, guided by precise specimen mapping and rapid frozen tissue processing. The Mohs map is central to the safety and accuracy of the procedure, is a critical communication tool among surgical team members, and is a key element in correlating tissue samples with the surgical site.
