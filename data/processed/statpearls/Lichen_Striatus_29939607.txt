# Lichen Striatus

**Authors:** Hasnain A. Syed, Radia T. Jamil, Kamleshun Ramphul

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Lichen striatus is an uncommon condition that occurs most often in children. This skin disorder presents as a pink rash with raised spots that merge to form one or more dull-red, slightly scaly linear bands following the Blaschko lines. These normally invisible lines may become apparent in conditions such as lichen striatus, also known as acquired blaschkoid dermatitis.
