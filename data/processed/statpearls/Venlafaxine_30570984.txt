# Venlafaxine

**Authors:** Dharminder Singh, Abdolreza Saadabadi

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Venlafaxine is categorized as a serotonin-norepinephrine reuptake inhibitor (SNRI). It is approved by the US Food and Drug Administration (FDA) to treat major depressive disorder (unipolar), social anxiety disorder, and panic disorder. In addition, venlafaxine is commonly used off-label for conditions such as attention-deficit disorder, fibromyalgia, diabetic neuropathy, complex pain syndromes, hot flashes, migraine prevention, posttraumatic stress disorder, obsessive-compulsive disorder, and premenstrual dysphoric disorder. Venlafaxine can be used either as a standalone treatment or as part of a combination therapy regimen with other medications.
