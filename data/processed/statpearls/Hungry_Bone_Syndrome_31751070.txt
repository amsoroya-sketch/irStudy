# Hungry Bone Syndrome

**Authors:** Carmen Cartwright, Catherine Anastasopoulou

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Hypocalcemia is an electrolyte imbalance that can have detrimental effects on a patient's health if not promptly identified and corrected. When evaluating a patient with hypocalcemia and considering the underlying causes, hungry bone syndrome (HBS) is often overlooked and underdiagnosed. HBS typically arises in the postoperative period following parathyroidectomy or thyroidectomy, although it may also occur in patients with osteoblastic metastases. Although HBS lacks a consensus definition, most sources describe it as profound hypocalcemia with a serum calcium level below 8.4 mg/dL that persists for more than 4 days in the postoperative period.
