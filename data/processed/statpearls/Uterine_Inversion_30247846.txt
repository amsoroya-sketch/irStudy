# Uterine Inversion

**Authors:** Monika Thakur, Angesh Thakur

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Uterine inversion is 1 of the most serious complications of childbirth. Uterine inversion refers to the collapse of the fundus into the uterine cavity. Although it does not often occur, it carries a high risk of mortality due to hemorrhage and shock.
