# Transesophageal Echocardiogram

**Authors:** Maria C. O'Rourke, Sheldon Goldstein, Byron R. Mendenhall

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Echocardiography uses ultrasound waves that obtain cardiac views of the heart and great vessels probed with an ultrasound probe. There are four basic rescue views that are probably the most useful in helping to determine the possible cause of the cardiac arrest and are all easily obtained.
