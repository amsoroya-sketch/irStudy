# Neonatal Hyperglycemia

**Authors:** Palanikumar Balasundaram, Manan Shah

**Source:** StatPearls - StatPearls Publishing

## Abstract

Neonatal hyperglycemia is commonly defined as serum glucose greater than 150 mg/dL (8.3 mmol/L) or whole-blood glucose greater than 125 mg/dL (6.9 mmol/L), independent of gestational or postmenstrual age. A safe blood glucose target for neonates ranges from 70 to 150 mg/dL, with the upper limit set by the renal glucose threshold in preterm infants. Classification depends on timing after birth: early-onset hyperglycemia occurs within the first 48 hours of life, and late-onset hyperglycemia develops after the initial 48 hours.
