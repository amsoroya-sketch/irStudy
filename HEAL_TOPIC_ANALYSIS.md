## Executive Summary

HEAL (Health Education Assets Library) contains **4,572 freely available medical education resources**, with a **heavy emphasis on hematology** (Albert Einstein College of Medicine Gallery of Hematology Images).

**Key Finding:** HEAL is **NOT comprehensive across all specialties**. It has exceptional depth in specific areas (hematology, anatomy, dermatology) but minimal/zero coverage in others (neurology, radiology, ophthalmology).
