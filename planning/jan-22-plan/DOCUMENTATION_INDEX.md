## 🎯 Key Decisions & Rationale

### Why MED-009 Psychiatry First?
- **Highest complexity** (MSE, risk assessment, Mental Health Act)
- **Establishes template** for other agents
- **Critical for AMC** (common topic)
- **Lessons learned** apply to all agents

### Why Week 1 Focus on Design?
- **QA-003 needs foundation** before scaling content
- **RAG integration patterns** need validation
- **Template-based approach** ensures consistency
- **Avoid technical debt** by designing upfront

### Why Three-Tier Validation?
- **Tier 1 (>0.90):** Auto-approve, no bottleneck
- **Tier 2 (0.75-0.90):** LLM verify ambiguous cases
- **Tier 3 (<0.75):** Reject and regenerate
- **Balance:** Speed (90% auto) + Quality (LLM verify)

### Why 100% Automation?
- **Scale:** 5,000 MCQs in 10 weeks impossible with manual review
- **Consistency:** Automated validation more consistent than human
- **Cost:** Zero human resource cost
- **Speed:** Real-time validation in generation pipeline
